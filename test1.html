Umrah Packages from Kerala with Skyway Tours and Travels

For every believer, the journey of Umrah is a deeply personal and spiritual milestone. At Skyway Tours and Travels in Nadapuram, Kerala, we understand that you don’t just need tickets and hotels — you need a partner who will take care of the details so you can focus on worship and reflection.

Our Umrah packages are carefully designed to offer comfort, convenience and peace of mind at every step: from your first enquiry in Kerala to your return home after visiting the Haramain.

  • Experienced local team in Kerala: Friendly staff based in Nadapuram to guide you through every step.
  • End–to–end support: From flights and hotels to local transport and basic guidance on the rituals.

  • Flexible options: Budget, standard and premium category packages available, suitable for families, couples and senior citizens.

What’s Included in Our Umrah Packages

In a typical Umrah package with Skyway Tours and Travels, you can expect:

  • Flights: Return economy-class air tickets from Kerala to Saudi Arabia (subject to airline and season).
  • Accommodation: Hotel stays in both Makkah and Madinah, with options close to the Haram depending on package type.
  • Transfers: Airport–hotel–airport transfers and intercity transfers between Makkah and Madinah.
  • Basic local assistance: Coordination at arrival, guidance to your hotel and orientation about nearby services.
  • Ground support: Contact numbers for assistance during your stay for coordination and emergency help.
  • Value add-ons (as per package): Ziyarah trips to important historical sites in Makkah and Madinah, subject to time and local regulations.

Exact inclusions can vary across groups and dates. When you contact us, we will share the latest package itinerary and price details for your travel month.

Sample 7-Day Umrah Itinerary

Below is an example of how a one-week Umrah trip may look. The exact sequence can differ based on flight schedules and the package you choose.

  1. Day 1 – Departure from Kerala & Arrival: Depart from Kerala and arrive in Jeddah or Madinah. Transfer to your hotel. Rest and adjust to the local climate and time.
  2. Day 2 – In Madinah: Visit Masjid an-Nabawi, spend time in prayer and salam, and explore the surrounding area. Optional ziyarah of nearby historical masajid and sites, depending on schedule.
  3. Day 3 – Madinah to Makkah: Check out from Madinah hotel, travel to Makkah. Enter into Ihram as per your chosen miqat and proceed for the first Umrah.
  4. Day 4 & 5 – Umrah & Ibadah in Makkah: Perform Tawaf, Sa’i between Safa and Marwah, and other acts of worship. Spend your time in Salah, Quran and dua near the Haram.
  5. Day 6 – Extra Worship / Optional Ziyarah: Free day for additional Tawaf, personal ibadah, and optional ziyarah of nearby important sites (if available in your package).
  6. Day 7 – Return to Kerala: Check out, transfer to the airport and fly back home with cherished memories and duas.

Types of Umrah Packages

We can customise trips based on your comfort level and budget:

  • Budget Umrah Packages: Comfortable hotels a little away from the Haram with shared transfers — ideal for budget–conscious pilgrims and groups.
  • Standard Umrah Packages: Popular mid-range hotels with convenient access to the Haram and balanced pricing.
  • Premium & Family Packages: Hotels very close to the Haram, more spacious rooms and additional assistance — perfect for senior citizens and families seeking extra comfort.

Share your preferred travel month, approximate dates, number of travellers and budget range, and we will match you with the most suitable option.

Important Things to Keep in Mind

  • Passport validity: Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
  • Health & fitness: Umrah involves walking and crowd movement, so inform us about any special needs or senior pilgrims in advance.
  • Modest packing: Keep Ihram clothes, comfortable footwear, a small bag, basic medicines and a refillable water bottle.
  • Learning the rituals: Familiarise yourself with the steps of Umrah before you travel so that you feel more confident on arrival.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I travel for Umrah with my family?

Yes, families are welcome and most of our groups include couples, children and elders. We can suggest rooming options (triple/quad sharing or inter-connecting rooms) based on your family size.

Do you assist with visas and documentation?

Yes, we guide you on the documentation required for Umrah–related travel and coordinate with our partners to help process your arrangements as per the prevailing rules at the time of booking.

Can senior citizens perform Umrah comfortably?

Many senior pilgrims travel every year. We recommend choosing packages with hotels close to the Haram and, where possible, arranging wheelchairs and slower-paced itineraries. Share your requirements with us so we can plan accordingly.

When is the best time to book?

Umrah seats and hotels can get busy during school vacations and Ramadan. It is always better to contact us well in advance of your preferred month so that you get better availability and prices.
