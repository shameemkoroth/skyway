15-Day Umrah Packages from Kerala with Skyway Tours and Travels

For every believer, the journey of Umrah is a deeply personal and spiritual milestone. At Skyway Tours and Travels in Nadapuram, Kerala, we understand that you don’t just need tickets and hotels — you need a partner who will take care of the details so you can focus on worship and reflection.

Our 15-day Umrah program is designed for pilgrims who prefer a relaxed pace, with ample time in both Madinah and Makkah for ibadah, rest and reflection, without rushing from one place to another.

Why Choose Skyway for Your 15-Day Umrah?

  • Experienced local team in Kerala: Friendly staff based in Nadapuram to guide you through every step.
  • End–to–end support: From flights and hotels to local transport and basic guidance on the rituals.
  • More time in Haramain: 15 days gives you more time to pray peacefully and repeat Umrah (where permitted under current rules).
  • Hotels close to the Haram: We prioritise properties that reduce walking time and fatigue so you can conserve energy for ibadah.
  • Flexible package categories: Budget, standard and premium options suitable for families, couples and senior citizens.
  • Transparent pricing: Clear inclusions and exclusions explained before booking — no last-minute surprises.
  • WhatsApp & phone support: Easy communication before and during your journey for any help you need.

What’s Included in Our Umrah Packages

In a typical 15-day Umrah package with Skyway Tours and Travels, you can expect:

  • Flights: Return economy-class air tickets from Kerala to Saudi Arabia (subject to airline and season).
  • Accommodation: Hotel stays in both Makkah and Madinah, with options close to the Haram depending on package type.
  • Transfers: Airport–hotel–airport transfers and intercity transfers between Makkah and Madinah.
  • Basic local assistance: Coordination at arrival, guidance to your hotel and orientation about nearby services.
  • Ground support: Contact numbers for assistance during your stay for coordination and emergency help.
  • Value add-ons (as per package): Ziyarah trips to important historical sites in Makkah and Madinah, subject to time and local regulations.

Exact inclusions can vary across groups and dates. When you contact us, we will share the latest 15-day package itinerary and price details for your travel month.

Sample 15-Day Umrah Itinerary

Below is an example of how a 15-day Umrah program may look. The exact sequence can differ based on flight schedules and the package you choose.

  1. Day 1 – Departure from Kerala & Arrival in Madinah: Depart from Kerala and arrive in Madinah. Transfer to your hotel, check-in and rest after the journey.
  2. Day 2 – First day in Madinah: Visit Masjid an-Nabawi, spend time in Salah and salam, get familiar with the surroundings.
  3. Day 3 – Ibadah in Madinah: Free time for ibadah, Quran recitation and personal duas in and around the Prophet’s Mosque.
  4. Day 4 – Optional Ziyarah in Madinah: Depending on package, visit important historical sites such as Quba Mosque, Uhud area and other notable locations.
  5. Day 5 – Continued Worship: Additional day in Madinah for relaxed worship and spiritual preparation before travelling to Makkah.
  6. Day 6 – Rest & Personal Time in Madinah: Light activities, shopping for essentials and getting ready for the journey to Makkah.
  7. Day 7 – Madinah to Makkah & First Umrah: Check out from Madinah, enter Ihram from the designated miqat and travel to Makkah. After reaching the hotel and a short rest, perform your first Umrah (Tawaf and Sa’i).
  8. Day 8 – Ibadah in Makkah: Spend the day in Salah, Tawaf and personal duas near the Ka’bah as per your capacity.
  9. Day 9 – Free Day for Worship: Continue ibadah at your own pace. Those who wish can repeat voluntary Tawaf and spend more time in the Haram.
  10. Day 10 – Optional Ziyarah in Makkah: Subject to package, visit nearby important sites around Makkah with your group.
  11. Day 11 – Rest & Reflection: Lighter day to rest, reflect, read Quran and make dua, especially for senior pilgrims.
  12. Day 12 – Extra Ibadah / Second Umrah (if allowed): Depending on current rules and your intention, those who wish may perform an additional Umrah or focus on increased worship.
  13. Day 13 – Shopping & Personal Time: Time for last-minute shopping, Zamzam arrangements (as per rules) and personal activities.
  14. Day 14 – Buffer Day: Extra day kept for flexibility – useful if there are any schedule changes or you simply want unhurried time in the Haram.
  15. Day 15 – Departure to Kerala: Check out from the hotel, transfer to the airport and fly back to Kerala with cherished memories and duas.

Share your preferred travel month, approximate dates, number of travellers and budget range, and we will match you with the most suitable 15-day option.

Important Things to Keep in Mind

  • Passport validity: Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months from the date of travel.
  • Health & fitness: Umrah involves walking and crowd movement, so inform us about any special needs or senior pilgrims in advance.
  • Modest packing: Keep Ihram clothes, comfortable footwear, a small bag, basic medicines and a refillable water bottle.
  • Learning the rituals: Familiarise yourself with the steps of Umrah before you travel so that you feel more confident on arrival.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is a 15-day Umrah package suitable for elders?

Yes. A 15-day program is often more comfortable for senior citizens because it avoids rushing. There is more time for rest between activities and more flexibility to adjust the day according to energy levels.

Do you assist with visas and documentation?

Yes, we guide you on the documentation required for Umrah–related travel and coordinate with our partners to help process your arrangements as per the prevailing rules at the time of booking.

Can families with children join the 15-day program?

Absolutely. Many families prefer a longer stay so children and elders can adjust comfortably. We can suggest rooming options (triple/quad sharing or inter-connecting rooms) based on your family size.

When should I book my 15-day Umrah?

Umrah seats and hotels can get busy during school vacations and Ramadan. It is always better to contact us well in advance of your preferred month so that you get better availability and prices.
