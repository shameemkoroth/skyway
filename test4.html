What is Umrah?

Umrah is an Islamic pilgrimage to the holy city of Mecca, Saudi Arabia, which can be performed at any time of the year, unlike the Hajj. It is often referred to as the "minor pilgrimage."

In Arabic, Umrah means "to visit a populated place." In the Sharia, it means performing Tawaf (circling the Kaaba) and Sa'i (walking between the hills of Safa and Marwah) while in a state of Ihram.

Spiritual Significance

Performing Umrah is a highly recommended Sunnah in Islam. It serves as a spiritual cleansing for the soul. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) said: "The performance of Umrah is an expiation for the sins committed (between it and the previous one)." It is a journey of devotion, repentance, and a chance to renew one's connection with Allah.

15 Days Complete Umrah Itinerary

A carefully planned schedule to ensure you perform your rituals with peace of mind and visit significant historical sites (Ziyarat).

Day 1: Departure & Arrival

Departure from India. Arrival at Jeddah Airport. Transport to Hotel in Makkah. Check-in, freshen up, and prepare for Umrah. Perform Umrah (Tawaf & Sa’i) under the guidance of our group leader.

Day 2: Rest & Ibadah in Makkah

Recover from travel fatigue. Dedicate the day to prayers (Salah) in Masjid al-Haram and reciting the Quran.

Day 3: Tawaf & Personal Worship

Engage in voluntary Tawaf. Explore the immediate vicinity of the Haram. Religious lectures (Bayan) may be organized by the group leader.

Day 4: Makkah Ziyarat (Historical Sites)

Visit holy sites including Jabal al-Nour (Cave of Hira), Jabal Thawr, the plains of Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Mina.

Day 5 - 7: Spiritual Reflection

Days dedicated to maximizing time in the Haram. Possibility of performing 2nd or 3rd Umrah (optional, starting from Masjid Aisha) depending on individual capacity.

Day 8: Journey to Madinah

Check out from Makkah hotel. Travel to Madinah Munawwarah by luxury bus or High-Speed Train (Haramain). Hotel Check-in at Madinah. Visit Masjid an-Nabawi to offer Salam to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

Day 9: Rawdah Visit

Scheduled slot for entering the Rawdah Riyad ul-Jannah (Garden of Paradise). This is managed via the Nusuk App appointments (we assist with this).

Day 10: Madinah Ziyarat

Guided tour of Madinah's historical sites: Masjid Quba (first mosque in Islam), Mount Uhud (site of the battle and martyrs' graves), and Masjid Qiblatayn.

Day 11 - 13: Ibadah in Madinah

Peaceful days spent in Masjid an-Nabawi. Focus on Durud Sharif and prayers. Visit local date markets and traditional souqs in free time.

Day 14: Preparation for Return

Packing luggage. Final visit to the Prophet’s Mosque to offer farewell Salam.

Day 15: Return Home

Transfer to Madinah or Jeddah Airport. Flight back to India with spiritual contentment and blessed memories.
