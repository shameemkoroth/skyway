The Essence of Umrah

Umrah is a pilgrimage to Makkah, Saudi Arabia, performed by Muslims that can be undertaken at any time of the year, in contrast to the Ḥajj which has specific dates. In Arabic, Umrah means "to visit a populated place". In the Sharia, it means performing Tawaf round the Ka'bah and Sa'i between Al-Safa and Al-Marwah, after assuming Ihram from a Miqat.

It is a spiritual cleansing, a way to ask for forgiveness, and a chance to renew your faith. At Sky Way Tours, we ensure your focus remains entirely on your Ibadah while we handle the travel, accommodation, and guidance.

The 4 Key Rituals

1
Ihram Entering the sacred state with pure intention (Niyyah) and specific clothing before crossing the Miqat.
2
Tawaf Circumambulating the Holy Ka'bah seven times in a counter-clockwise direction.
3
Sa'i Walking seven times between the hills of Safa and Marwah, commemorating Hajar (AS).
4
Tahallul Exiting the state of Ihram by shaving (Halq) or trimming (Taqsir) the hair.

15-Day Detailed Schedule

1
Departure & 1st Umrah Jeddah / Makkah
  • Departure from Kerala (CCJ/COK/CNN).
  • Wear Ihram from Airport or in Flight before Miqat.
  • Arrival at Jeddah, transfer to Makkah Hotel by Bus.
  • Perform Umrah: Tawaf, Sa'i, and Tahallul with group Muallim.
2
Rest & Prayers Makkah
  • Recover from travel fatigue.
  • Focus on 5 daily prayers in Masjid Al-Haram.
  • Evening orientation regarding Makkah stay.
3
Makkah Ziyarat Makkah
  • Morning bus trip to holy sites.
  • Jabal Al-Nour: View the mountain of Cave Hira.
  • Jabal Thawr: The cave where Prophet (PBUH) hid.
  • Drive through Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Mina.
4
Optional Umrah / Taneem Makkah
  • Day free for personal Ibadah.
  • Those wishing to do 2nd Umrah can visit Masjid Aisha (Taneem) to assume Ihram.
5
Jumu'ah in Haram Makkah
  • Attend Friday prayers early to get a spot inside the Mataf or shaded areas.
  • Recitation of Surah Al-Kahf.
6
Souq & Shopping Makkah
  • Explore local markets near Clock Tower.
  • Buy Zamzam cans (if arranging cargo) or gifts.
7
Tawaf Al-Wida Makkah
  • Perform the Farewell Tawaf before leaving Makkah.
  • Pack bags for the journey to Madina Munawwarah.
8
Transfer to Madina Bus Travel
  • Checkout and travel by Luxury AC Bus (approx 5-6 hours).
  • Check-in to Madina Hotel near Markaziya.
  • Visit Masjid An-Nabawi for Salam to the Prophet (PBUH).
9
Rawdah Visit Madina
  • Enter the Rawdah (Riyad ul-Jannah) based on the Nusuk App appointment / Group Slot.
  • Offer 2 Rakaat Nafl prayer.
10
Madina Ziyarat Madina
  • Masjid Quba: Offer 2 Rakaat (Reward of Umrah).
  • Mount Uhud: Visit the Archers' mount and Shuhada cemetery.
  • Masjid Qiblatain: Mosque of two Qiblas.
11
Dates Farm & Khandaq Madina
  • Visit the site of the Battle of Trench (Seven Mosques).
  • Visit a lush Date Farm to buy fresh Madina Dates.
12
Spiritual Reflection Madina
  • Spend maximum time in the Prophet's Mosque.
  • Recite Quran and excessive Durood (Salawat).
13
Jannat Al-Baqi Madina
  • Visit the Baqi Cemetery adjacent to the Mosque (usually open after Asr).
  • Pay respects to the Sahaba and family of the Prophet buried there.
14
Final Salam Madina
  • Offer Final Salam.
  • Pack luggage, weigh bags, and prepare for departure.
15
Return Home Airport
  • Transfer to Madina (MED) or Jeddah (JED) Airport.
  • Flight back to Kerala with spiritual contentment.
