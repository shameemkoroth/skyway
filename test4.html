15-Day Umrah Itinerary

A day-by-day breakdown of your spiritual journey with Sky Way Tours. We balance worship, education, and rest to ensure a fulfilling pilgrimage.

Detailed Schedule

1
Departure & Arrival Jeddah / Makkah
  • Departure from Kerala (CCJ/COK/CNN).
  • Arrival at Jeddah International Airport.
  • Transfer to Makkah Hotel by AC Bus.
  • Check-in and freshen up.
  • Perform 1st Umrah under the guidance of our Muallim.
2
Rest & Ibadah Makkah
  • Rest after the journey and Umrah rituals.
  • Focus on daily 5-time prayers in Masjid Al-Haram.
  • Evening talk/orientation by Group Leader.
3
Makkah Ziyarat (Sightseeing) Makkah
  • Morning trip by bus to historical sites.
  • Visit Jabal Al-Nour (Cave Hira), Jabal Thawr.
  • Visit Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Mina grounds.
  • Return to hotel for Dhuhr prayer.
4
Free Day / Optional Umrah Makkah
  • Day dedicated to personal worship (Tawaf, Quran recitation).
  • Optional: Visit Masjid Aisha (Taneem) for those wishing to perform 2nd Umrah on their own.
5
Jumu'ah / Ibadah Makkah
  • Early arrival at Haram for Jumu'ah Prayer (if Friday falls here).
  • Spend the day in the Haram courtyard.
6
Shopping & Local Exploration Makkah
  • Visit local markets near the Clock Tower for souvenirs.
  • Group gathering after Isha prayer.
7
Tawaf Al-Wida & Preparation Makkah
  • Perform Farewell Tawaf (Tawaf Al-Wida).
  • Pack luggage for the journey to Madina.
8
Journey to Madina Travel
  • Morning checkout from Makkah Hotel.
  • Travel to Madina by Luxury AC Bus (approx. 5-6 hours).
  • Hotel Check-in near Masjid An-Nabawi.
  • Visit the Prophet's Mosque for greeting (Salam).
9
Rawdah Visit (Scheduled) Madina
  • Appointment based entry to the Rawdah (Riadhul Jannah).
  • Please follow the specific time slot given by the group leader.
  • Rest of the day for prayers.
10
Madina Ziyarat Madina
  • Morning tour of historical sites.
  • Visit Masjid Quba (First mosque in Islam - Reward of an Umrah).
  • Visit Mount Uhud (Site of the battle).
  • Visit Masjid Qiblatain.
11
Date Farm Visit Madina
  • Visit a local Date Farm.
  • Purchase fresh Madina dates (Ajwa, Sukari, etc.).
  • Free time for personal Ibadah.
12
Religious Lectures / Rest Madina
  • Short class/bayan by scholar regarding the significance of Madina.
  • Focus on Salawat (Durood) upon the Prophet (PBUH).
13
Free Day Madina
  • Visit Jannat Al-Baqi (near the Prophet's Mosque) after Asr prayer (if open).
  • Final shopping for gifts.
14
Final Salam & Packing Madina
  • Offer final prayers and Salam to the Prophet (PBUH).
  • Pack luggage and weigh bags (check airline limits).
15
Departure Airport Transfer
  • Check out from Madina Hotel.
  • Transfer to Madina Airport (MED) or Jeddah Airport (JED) depending on flight.
  • Flight back to Kerala.

Package Inclusions Summary

Visa & Flight

Tourist/Umrah Visa processing and Round-trip economy class air tickets included.

Hotels

Stay in comfortable hotels within walking distance to Haram in both cities.

Kerala Food

Full board buffet meals (Kerala Style) served daily.

ℹ️ Note on Itinerary

This itinerary is subject to change based on flight schedules and sighting of the moon (for Islamic dates). Actual travel days may vary slightly.
