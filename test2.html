Saudi Arabia Visa Guide for Indians (2025 Updates)

Saudi Arabia has rapidly become a top destination for both pilgrimage and leisure. Whether you are planning Umrah, a business trip to Riyadh, or a holiday in AlUla, choosing the right visa is crucial. Skyway Tours & Travels simplifies the process with up-to-date guidance on the new rules.

⚠️ Important for Standard Sticker Visas

Note for 2025: While E-Visas are usually Multiple Entry (1 Year), standard sticker visas applied through VFS Tasheel for Indian nationals are increasingly being issued as Single Entry (30 Days) based on travel itinerary. Ensure your travel plans align with the visa type granted.

Select the Right Visa for You

There are currently three main ways for Indian citizens to enter Saudi Arabia:

1. The 96-Hour Stopover Visa (Transit)

Best for short Umrah trips or quick city breaks. This is the most cost-effective option.

  • Eligibility: Open to all, but you MUST fly via Saudia Airlines or Flynas.
  • Cost: The visa fee itself is Free. However, you must pay an administrative & medical insurance fee (approx. 40 SAR to 100 SAR total).
  • Duration: Valid for a stay of up to 96 hours (4 Days).
  • Umrah: Allowed. You can perform Umrah and visit the Prophet's Mosque (Rawdah requires booking).

2. E-Visa / Visa on Arrival (Conditional)

The fastest option, but strict eligibility rules apply.

  • Who is Eligible? Indian nationals who hold a valid US, UK, or Schengen business/tourist visa.
  • Crucial Rule: The US/UK/Schengen visa must have been used at least once (entry stamp required) to enter that country/region before arriving in Saudi Arabia.
  • Validity: Typically 1 Year, Multiple Entry (Stay up to 90 days).

3. Standard Tourist/Visit Visa (Sticker)

For those who do not qualify for the above two options.

  • Process: Requires document submission at the VFS Tasheel center (Passport, Photos, Bank Statements, Employment Proof).
  • Processing Time: Usually 4-5 working days.
  • Assistance: Skyway Tours handles the appointment booking, form filling, and document verification for you.

🕌 Umrah Rules for Tourists

You DO NOT need a specific "Umrah Visa" anymore. You can perform Umrah on any Tourist, Visit, or Stopover visa.

  • Nusuk App: Download this app to book your slot for Rawdah (visiting the grave of Prophet Muhammad PBUH in Medina). Umrah slots in Mecca are generally open for tourists without booking, but checking the app is recommended during peak seasons.

Top Destinations to Explore

AlUla (Hegra)

Saudi's first UNESCO site. Ancient Nabataean tombs carved into massive sandstone mountains. A living museum of history.

Historic Jeddah

Explore 'Al-Balad', famous for its coral-stone buildings and traditional wooden 'Roshan' balconies near the Red Sea coast.

The Red Sea Project

Newly opened luxury island destinations offering untouched coral reefs, turquoise waters, and world-class diving experiences.

Diriyah

Located in Riyadh, this is the birthplace of the Saudi state. The At-Turaif district offers luxury dining and deep cultural history.
