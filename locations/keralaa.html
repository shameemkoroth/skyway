🌴 Kerala: God's Own Country

Welcome to Kerala, a land of unparalleled natural beauty where emerald backwaters, lush tea-covered hills, and golden beaches converge. Famously known as 'God's Own Country', Kerala offers a unique blend of historical charm, cultural richness, and holistic wellness.

📋 Essential Travel Facts

  • Best Time to Visit: Winter (Oct - Mar) — Pleasant for sightseeing, backwaters, and beaches.
  • Ideal Duration: 7 to 10 Days (To cover major highlights like Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady, and Alleppey).
  • Airports: Cochin, Thiruvananthapuram, Calicut International Airports.
  • Official Language: Malayalam. English is widely spoken in tourist areas.

🗺️ Top Tourist Destinations

  • Alleppey: Experience a night in traditional houseboats through the serene backwaters.
  • Munnar: Explore misty tea plantations and scenic hill views.
  • Kochi: Visit Fort Kochi, Chinese Fishing Nets, and colonial landmarks.
  • Thekkady: Spot elephants in Periyar National Park and walk through spice gardens.
  • Varkala & Kovalam: Relax on stunning beaches with dramatic cliffs and sunsets.

🎭 Culture & Wellness

  • Kathakali: Witness this colorful traditional dance-drama of Kerala.
  • Kalaripayattu: Watch ancient martial arts performances.
  • Ayurveda: Experience authentic treatments and rejuvenation therapy.
  • Festivals: Onam, Snake Boat Races, and more cultural celebrations.

🍽️ Culinary Delights

  • Sadya: A vegetarian feast on banana leaves.
  • Karimeen Pollichathu: Pearl spot fish wrapped in banana leaves.
  • Appam & Stew: Soft rice pancakes with coconut milk stew.
  • Malabar Biryani: Fragrant, flavorful, and unique to Kerala.
