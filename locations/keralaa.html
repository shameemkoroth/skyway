🌴 Kerala: God's Own Country

Welcome to Kerala, a land of unparalleled natural beauty where emerald backwaters, lush tea-covered hills, and golden beaches converge. Famously known as 'God's Own Country', Kerala offers a unique blend of historical charm, cultural richness, and holistic wellness.

📋 Essential Travel Facts

  • Best Time to Visit: Winter (Oct - Mar) – Pleasant for sightseeing, backwaters, and beaches.
  • Ideal Duration: 7 to 10 Days to cover Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady, and Alleppey.
  • Airports: Cochin (COK), Thiruvananthapuram (TRV), Calicut (CCJ).
  • Language: Malayalam (English widely spoken).

🗺️ Top Destinations & Experiences

  • Alleppey: Houseboat cruises through backwaters and paddy fields.
  • Munnar: Misty hills and tea plantations.
  • Kochi: Fort Kochi, Jewish Synagogue, and colonial charm.
  • Thekkady: Wildlife safaris in Periyar National Park.
  • Varkala & Kovalam: Iconic beaches with stunning sunsets.

🎭 Culture & Wellness

  • Kathakali: Classical dance-drama with expressive storytelling.
  • Kalaripayattu: World’s oldest martial art.
  • Ayurveda: Ideal during monsoon (Jun–Sep).
  • Festivals: Onam and Snake Boat Races.

🍽️ Culinary Delights

  • Sadya: Traditional feast on banana leaf.
  • Karimeen Pollichathu: Spiced pearl spot fish.
  • Appam & Stew: Rice pancakes with coconut stew.
  • Kozhikode Biryani: Fragrant and flavorful.
