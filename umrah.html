Package Snapshot: 15 Days / 14 Nights

Our popular 15-day itinerary provides ample time to perform Umrah, engage in prayers at the Holy Mosques, and explore the historical sites of Islam.

The Spiritual Itinerary

  1. Arrival & Umrah:
    Arrive in Jeddah and transfer to Makkah. After checking in, you will be guided to Masjid Al-Haram to perform your Umrah rituals (Tawaf, Sa’i, and Tahallul).
  2. Days in Makkah & Ziyarat:
    Spend your days in prayer at the Kaaba. We organize guided Ziyarat to significant sites such as Jabal Al-Noor (Cave of Hira), Jabal Thawr, and the plains of Arafat and Mina.
  3. Journey to Madinah:
    Travel comfortably to the City of the Prophet (PBUH) via private AC coach.
  4. Days in Madinah:
    Offer your Salam at the Rawdah Rasool in Masjid Al-Nabawi. Our team will take you to visit Masjid Quba, Masjid Al-Qiblatain, and Mount Uhud.
  5. Return:
    With a heart full of peace and memories of the Holy Land, you will be transferred to the airport for your return flight.
