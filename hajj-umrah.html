Hajj & Umrah Services

Embark on the most significant spiritual journey of your life with Skyway Tours & Travels. We understand that a pilgrimage to the Holy Lands is not just a trip; it is an act of worship and a lifelong dream. Our dedicated team ensures your journey is comfortable, compliant with regulations, and spiritually fulfilling.

🌟 Customized Pilgrimage Planning

We do not believe in rigid "Economy" or "Premium" boxes. Every pilgrim has unique needs. We build customized itineraries based on your preferred travel dates, budget, proximity to the Haram, and airline preference.

⚠️ A Note on Pricing

Rates for flights, hotels, and visas fluctuate daily based on the season (Ramadan/Hajj season), exchange rates, and availability. We provide real-time quotes to ensure you get the best current market rate without hidden fees.

Our Comprehensive Services

We handle the logistics so you can focus on your Ibadah. Our end-to-end support includes:

1. Umrah Visa Processing

We assist with the issuance of the electronic Umrah Visa or Tourist Visa (for eligible purposes), ensuring all documentation meets the Ministry of Hajj & Umrah requirements.

2. Flight Bookings

Whether you prefer direct flights to Jeddah/Medina or connecting flights to save costs, we book confirmed tickets on major carriers including Saudia, Flynas, Emirates, Etihad, and Air India.

3. Accommodation (Hotels)

From 5-star luxury hotels with Haram views to comfortable budget-friendly hotels with shuttle services, we secure bookings that match your budget and comfort level.

4. Ground Transportation

  • Private Transfers: GMC, Sedans, or SUVs for families.
  • Group Transfers: Comfortable Coaster buses.
  • Haramain Train: Booking assistance for the high-speed train between Makkah and Medina.

Ziyarat (Historical Tours)

No journey is complete without visiting the historical sites associated with the Prophet (PBUH). We arrange guided Ziyarat tours in both Holy Cities.

Masjid Al Haram (Makkah)

The focal point of every Muslim's life. We ensure your accommodation allows for easy access to the Haram for daily prayers and Tawaf.

Masjid Al Nabawi (Medina)

Visit the Prophet's Mosque and the Rawdah. We assist with the Nusuk app appointments required for entering the Rawdah Sharif.

Masjid Quba

The first mosque built by the Prophet (PBUH). Offering two Rakaat here holds the reward of a performed Umrah.

Jabal Al-Noor (Hira Cave)

The mountain housing the Cave of Hira, where the Prophet (PBUH) received the first revelation of the Quran.

Mount Uhud & Archers' Hill

A site of great historical significance. Visit the graves of the Martyrs of Uhud, including Hamza (RA).

Jabal Thawr

The cave where the Prophet (PBUH) and Abu Bakr (RA) took shelter during their migration (Hijrah) to Medina.

Documents Required

To initiate your Umrah booking, we generally require the following:

  1. Passport: Original passport with at least 6 months validity from the date of travel.
  2. Photographs: 2 passport-sized photos with a white background.
  3. Pan Card / Aadhar: Copy for Indian citizens (Required for TCS purposes).
  4. Vaccination Certificate: Covid-19 vaccination proof (if currently required by regulations).
