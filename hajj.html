Hajj & Umrah Group Package from Kerala

All arrangements are subject to the latest Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah regulations and airline rules. Dates, routing and hotel distance are confirmed at the time of booking based on your chosen departure.

Tentative Itinerary Snapshot

The final day–wise program will depend on confirmed Hajj dates, airline schedule and hotel allocation. Below is a general idea of how a combined Hajj & Umrah group program typically looks:

  1. Day 1–2: Departure from Kerala – Arrival Madinah
    Group meets at the airport in Kerala, completes check-in and immigration. Arrival in Madinah, transfer to hotel, rest and first visit to Masjid an-Nabawi.
  2. Day 3–5: Stay in Madinah – Ziyarah
    Regular prayers in Masjid an-Nabawi, guided ziyarah to Quba Mosque, Uhud, Qiblatain and other important sites.
  3. Day 6: Madinah to Makkah – First Umrah
    Travel to Makkah in Ihram. On arrival, perform first Umrah (Tawaf and Sa’i) with group guidance.
  4. Day 7–8: Makkah Stay – Ibadah & Ziyarah
    Optional additional Umrah (subject to physical ability), individual ibadah, and ziyarah to local sites.
  5. Day 9–13: Hajj Days – Mina, Arafat & Muzdalifah
    Movement to Mina, standing at Arafat, stay at Muzdalifah, stoning of Jamarat and Tawaf-e-Ziyarat – as per the guidance of the group leader and Saudi regulations.
  6. Day 14–15: Final Days in Makkah
    Ibadah near Haram, any remaining rites (if applicable) and preparation for return journey.
  7. Day 16: Return to Kerala
    Check-out, airport transfer, flight back to Kerala with unforgettable memories and duas.

Note: The above is only an indicative flow. Exact number of days, sequence and services will be as per the final package confirmation for your chosen batch.

Eligibility & Documents

Please keep the following documents ready when you enquire / register:

  • Valid Indian passport with at least 6 months validity and sufficient blank pages.
  • Recent passport-size photographs as per Saudi requirements (white background).
  • Copy of Aadhaar and PAN card.
  • Proof of relationship for family members (if required).
  • Basic medical fitness and any vaccination certificates required by Saudi authorities at the time of travel.

For Hajj, pilgrims must also be eligible as per Saudi quota and age/health guidelines. Exact conditions can change – our team will share the latest requirements when you enquire.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many package types do you offer?

For this program, we keep it simple with one standard group package. The entire group follows the same plan and enjoys the same service level.

Are room options (double / triple / quad) available?

This program is designed as a single, unified package. Room sharing is arranged in a practical way for families and individuals within the same overall standard.

Is this package suitable for senior citizens?

Many elderly pilgrims perform Hajj & Umrah every year. As the rituals involve walking and standing, we recommend that senior citizens and those with health concerns consult their doctor before booking. Where possible, we try to allocate rooms and bus seating with extra care for seniors.

Can I join only for Umrah and not for Hajj?

Depending on the season, we may have group programs focused purely on Umrah outside the Hajj period. Please contact us with your preferred month, and our team will confirm the available options.

Will you help with visa and forms?

Yes. We provide complete visa assistance including online forms, documentation guidance and coordination with authorized partners. Final approval of visa remains with the respective authorities.
