Mysore & Coorg

A journey from the City of Palaces to the Scotland of India. Experience royalty, mist-covered hills, and endless coffee plantations.

Vibe

Heritage & Nature

Royal Mysore

City of Palaces

  • Mysore Palace: One of India's most grandiose royal residences, famous for its illumination.
  • Brindavan Gardens: Iconic terrace gardens with a musical fountain show.
  • Chamundi Hill: Ancient temple offering panoramic city views.

Misty Coorg

Scotland of India

  • Coffee Plantations: Stay in homestays surrounded by lush estates and spice gardens.
  • Abbey Falls: A stunning waterfall located amidst coffee bushes.
  • Dubare Camp: An elephant training camp where you can bathe and feed elephants.

Culture & Nature

Highlights of the Southern Circuit

Golden Temple

The Namdroling Monastery in Bylakuppe (Coorg), the largest Tibetan settlement in South India.

Raja's Seat

A seasonal garden of flowers and artificial fountains offering a breathtaking view of the valley sunset.

Silk & Sandalwood

Shop for authentic Mysore Silk Sarees and fragrant Sandalwood products in the bustling city markets.

Karnataka Flavors

  • Mysore Masala Dosa: Crisp dosa with a spicy red chutney smear.
  • Mysore Pak: A melt-in-the-mouth sweet made of ghee and gram flour.
  • Filter Coffee: Freshly brewed strong coffee, best enjoyed in Coorg.

Travel Tips

Wear comfortable shoes for walking palace grounds.

Carry light woolens for Coorg evenings.

Respect dress codes at temples and monasteries.

Buy coffee and spices from authorized estates.
