Top Attractions

Must-visit locations in the archipelago

Havelock

Radhanagar Beach

Voted one of Asia’s best beaches, known for its powdery white sand and crystal-clear turquoise waters.

Port Blair

Cellular Jail

A national memorial that tells the poignant story of India’s freedom struggle. A must-visit for history buffs.

North Bay

North Bay Island

The hub for water sports. Perfect for snorkeling, sea walking, and viewing vibrant coral reefs.

Adventure & Sports

  • Scuba Diving Explore the vibrant reefs of Havelock & Neil.
  • Snorkeling Experience coral gardens up close at North Bay.
  • Mangrove Kayaking Paddle through serene creeks, even at night!

Best Time to Visit

The ideal time is between October and May. The weather is perfect for swimming and sightseeing, with calm seas and clear skies. Avoid the monsoon season (June-Sept) for water sports.

Nature & Wildlife

Home to rare flora and fauna. Explore the Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park for biodiversity or trek through Mount Harriet National Park for panoramic views.
