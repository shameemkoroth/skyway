MOUNTAINS & VALLEYS

Himachal Pradesh

Escape to the snow-clad peaks of Manali and the colonial charm of Shimla. The perfect mountain getaway awaits.

Essential Travel Facts

Best Time

Mar-Jun (Summer)
Dec-Feb (Snow)

Duration

5 - 7 Days

Access

Chandigarh (IXC)
Delhi (DEL)

Clothing

Heavy Woolens (Winter)
Light Jackets (Summer)

Shimla

Queen of Hills

  • The Ridge: Visit Christ Church and enjoy panoramic views.
  • Mall Road: The heart of Shimla for shopping and cafes.
  • Kufri: Famous for yak rides and snow fun in winter.

Manali

Valley of Gods

  • Solang Valley: Hub for paragliding, skiing, and ATVs.
  • Atal Tunnel: Gateway to Lahaul & Sissu waterfalls.
  • Hadimba Temple: Historic wooden temple in the forest.

Adventure & Culture

Experiences beyond the views

River Rafting

Experience the thrill of white water rafting on the Beas river in Kullu.

Sissu Valley

Cross the Atal Tunnel to witness the stunning Sissu waterfall and landscapes.

Paragliding

Soar above Solang Valley for a breathtaking aerial view of the Himalayas.

Mountain Food

  • Siddu: Steamed wheat bread stuffed with spices.
  • Himachali Dham: A traditional festive meal on a leaf plate.
  • Pahadi Maggi: Noodles with a view – a mountain classic.

Travel Tips

Carry motion sickness meds for winding roads.

Book Rohtang Pass permits in advance.

Wear comfortable shoes with good grip.

Stay hydrated at high altitudes.
