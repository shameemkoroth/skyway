GOD'S OWN COUNTRY

Kerala, India

A land of emerald backwaters, lush tea hills, and golden beaches. Experience serenity, Ayurveda, and culture.

Nature's Masterpiece

Kerala is a tropical paradise where you can cruise through the backwaters of Alleppey one day and trek through the misty tea gardens of Munnar the next. It is world-renowned for its Ayurveda treatments and vibrant festivals like Onam.

Top Attractions

The Jewels of the South

Alleppey

Backwaters

Cruise through a labyrinth of canals on a traditional Kettuvallam (houseboat). A must-do experience.

Munnar

Munnar

Rolling hills carpeted in emerald green tea plantations. Perfect for trekking and enjoying cool mountain air.

Kochi

Fort Kochi

A historic port city known for its colonial architecture, Jewish Synagogue, and iconic Chinese fishing nets.

Culture & Flavors

  • Sadya A grand vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf.
  • Ayurveda Rejuvenate with world-famous therapeutic massages.
  • Karimeen Pollichathu Pearl Spot fish marinated and grilled in banana leaf.

Best Time to Visit

The peak season is October to March for pleasant weather. The Monsoon (June-Sept) is considered the best time for Ayurvedic treatments.

Art Forms

Don't miss a Kathakali performance, a classical dance-drama known for its vibrant makeup, or Kalaripayattu, the ancient martial art of Kerala.
