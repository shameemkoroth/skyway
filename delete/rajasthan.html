The Royal Cities

Icons of Rajputana Architecture

Jaipur (Pink City)

Capital city famous for Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, and City Palace.

Highlights: Forts & Shopping

Udaipur (Lake City)

Romantic city of lakes. Visit Lake Pichola and the City Palace.

Highlights: Lakes & Luxury

Jaisalmer (Golden City)

A living fort city in the desert. Famous for Sam Sand Dunes and camel safaris.

Highlights: Desert & Camping

Jodhpur (Blue City)

Dominated by the massive Mehrangarh Fort and blue-painted houses.

Highlights: Architecture

Rajasthani Cuisine

  • Dal Baati Churma: The signature wholesome dish of the state.
  • Laal Maas: A fiery meat curry cooked with red chillies.
  • Ghewar: A sweet disc-shaped cake, popular during festivals.

Travel Tips

Carry sunglasses and sunscreen.

Wear comfortable walking shoes for forts.

Respect local customs at temples.

Keep a jacket for cool desert nights.
