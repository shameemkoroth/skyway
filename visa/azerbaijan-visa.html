Visa Types for Indian Nationals

Azerbaijan has simplified its entry process for Indians significantly. The most popular and convenient option is the electronic visa system.

1. Standard Tourist eVisa (ASAN Visa)

  • Purpose: Tourism, short business meetings, visiting friends or family.
  • Validity: Valid for 90 days from the date of issue.
  • Stay Duration: Allows a stay of up to 30 days.
  • Entry Type: Single Entry only. If you leave the country, you must apply for a new visa to re-enter.

2. Urgent eVisa

  • Purpose: For travelers with immediate travel plans.
  • Processing: Expedited processing, typically approved within hours.

3. Visa on Arrival (Airport Only)

  • Note: While Indian citizens are currently eligible for Visa on Arrival at international airports in Azerbaijan, we strongly recommend obtaining an eVisa before departure. This avoids long queues at immigration, payment hassles at the airport, and potential boarding denials by airlines checking for return tickets and visas.

Essential Documents Required

The documentation for an Azerbaijan eVisa is straightforward. Skyway Tours ensures your digital scans meet the strict government quality standards.

Mandatory Documents

  • Passport Scan: A clear, coloured scan of the first and last bio-pages of your passport. The passport must be valid for at least 3 months after the expiration date of the visa.
  • Photograph: While often retrieved from the passport, a separate digital passport-sized photo (white background) is recommended to have on hand.

Documents Recommended for Immigration

While the eVisa application only requires the passport, immigration officers at Baku airport may ask for:

  • Flight Tickets: Confirmed return or onward journey tickets.
  • Hotel Booking: Proof of accommodation for the duration of your stay.
  • Travel Insurance: Highly recommended for medical coverage during your trip.

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Registration Requirement: If you intend to stay in Azerbaijan for more than 15 days, you must register with the State Migration Service. Hotels usually handle this, but if you are staying in an Airbnb/Apartment, you must ensure this is done to avoid heavy fines upon exit.
  • Nagorno-Karabakh: Entry to the Nagorno-Karabakh region requires special government permission. Unauthorized entry is illegal.
  • Print Your Visa: Always carry a physical colour printout of your eVisa, as immigration officers will stamp your passport and may ask to see the paper copy.

Common Questions

Can I extend my tourist visa?

Tourist eVisas are generally non-extendable. You must leave the country before your 30 days are up to avoid penalties.

Do I need a transit visa for a layover in Baku?

If you do not leave the international transit area of the airport, a visa is not required. If you plan to exit the airport for a city tour, you must obtain a valid eVisa.

What happens if my passport expires in less than 6 months?

Azerbaijan strictly requires validity of at least 3 months beyond the visa expiry date. If your passport is nearing expiry, we advise renewing it before applying.
