Azerbaijan Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

From the modern Flame Towers of Baku to the ancient fire temples of Ateshgah, Azerbaijan is a land of stunning contrasts. Skyway Tours & Travels simplifies your entry with the official ASAN E-Visa, ensuring you avoid common pitfalls and meet all the latest 2025 entry regulations.

⚠️ Important Rule: 15-Day Registration

If you plan to stay in Azerbaijan for more than 15 days, you MUST register with the State Migration Service. Failure to do so will result in a hefty fine at the airport upon departure. We assist our clients with this mandatory process.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. ASAN Electronic Visa (Standard)

The standard tourist visa for Indian citizens. It is applied for online without visiting any embassy.

  • Validity: Single Entry, valid for 90 days from issue.
  • Stay Duration: Maximum 30 Days per visit.
  • Processing Time: Standard (3-4 Days) or Urgent (3-5 Hours).
  • Eligibility: All Indian passport holders.

2. Visa on Arrival (Conditional)

Generally, Indians DO NOT get Visa on Arrival. However, there is one major exception:

  • Eligibility: You qualify for Visa on Arrival ONLY IF you hold a valid Residence Visa for the UAE (valid for at least 6 months).
  • Note: If you live in India and do not have a UAE residency, you MUST get the E-Visa before flying.

Essential Documents Required (E-Visa)

We ensure your application is error-free to guarantee approval. You will need:

  • Passport Scan: Valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.
  • Photograph: Clear digital photo.
  • Medical Insurance: Highly Recommended. While strictly not always asked, it is officially advised by the embassy to carry travel insurance.
  • Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation (required for registration if staying >15 days).

🇦🇿 The Land of Fire: Main Attractions

Azerbaijan offers a mix of ultra-modern architecture and Silk Road history. Here are the must-visit spots:

Baku Old City (Icherisheher)

A UNESCO World Heritage site. Wander through the medieval walled city, visit the Maiden Tower, and see the Palace of the Shirvanshahs.

Flame Towers & Highland Park

Witness the iconic trio of skyscrapers that light up like flames at night. Highland Park offers the best panoramic view of the Baku skyline.

Gobustan Mud Volcanoes

Azerbaijan is home to half of the world's mud volcanoes. Visit the bubbling mud fields and the ancient petroglyphs (rock carvings) nearby.

Gabala (Qabala)

The "Switzerland of Azerbaijan." Famous for the Tufandag Mountain Resort (skiing/cable cars), lush forests, and the scenic Nohur Lake.

Sheki & Khan's Palace

A historic Silk Road city. The Palace of Sheki Khans is a masterpiece of stained glass (Shebeke) and intricate frescoes without a single nail.

Ateshgah (Fire Temple)

Located in Surakhani, this castle-like temple was a place of worship for Zoroastrians, built atop a natural gas vent that fueled eternal flames.

Common Questions

Is it safe for Indian tourists?

Yes, Azerbaijan is very safe and tourist-friendly. Baku is known for its low crime rate and helpful locals.

Can I get a Visa on Arrival if I have a US Visa?

No. Unlike some other countries, a US Visa does not grant Visa on Arrival in Azerbaijan. Only a UAE Residence Visa works for VOA.

What currency should I carry?

The currency is the **Azerbaijani Manat (AZN)**. It is best to carry USD or Euros to exchange in Baku. Indian Rupees are generally not accepted.
