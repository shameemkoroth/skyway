Bahrain Visa — Complete Guide

Sky Way Tours & Travels provides end-to-end Bahrain visa assistance for Indian travellers. Below you'll find every detail you need — visa types, eligibility, required documents, fees, processing times, how we help, and the most common FAQs.

Overview

Bahrain offers several visa routes depending on purpose: Tourist (eVisa), Business, Family/Visit, Work (employment/residence), and special categories such as transit and diplomatic visas. Most short-term visitor applications are processed via Bahrain’s official eVisa platform; longer stay and employment visas require sponsorship from a Bahraini employer or family sponsor.

Types of Bahrain Visas

1. Tourist / Visitor eVisa

Electronic visas (eVisas) are available for tourism, family visits and short business trips. They are typically issued as single-entry or multiple-entry and may have validity options (for example, 14/30/90 days depending on the visa class). Most applicants apply using the Bahrain eVisa portal or via an authorized travel agent.

2. Business Visa

For short business meetings, conferences, or negotiations. Often requires an invitation or letter from the Bahraini company and may be processed through the company sponsor or the eVisa system depending on the case.

3. Work / Employment Visa

Requires employer sponsorship. The employer applies for a work permit and issues an entry visa; after arrival the employee completes residency formalities. Work visas normally lead to residence permits.

4. Family / Visit Visa

Issued for visiting relatives or for family members of Bahrain residents (sponsored by the resident). Family visas do not automatically permit work — a separate work permit is required to be employed.

Eligibility — Who Needs a Visa?

Most Indian passport holders require a visa (eVisa or stamped visa) before travel — make sure your passport has at least 6 months validity beyond intended stay. Some travellers may be eligible for visa-on-arrival under specific conditions; always check eligibility before travel.

Standard Documents Required

  • Passport (valid ≥ 6 months) — scanned colour copy
  • Recent passport-size photograph (as per specs)
  • Completed online visa application form
  • Confirmed return or onward flight ticket
  • Proof of accommodation (hotel booking or sponsor letter)
  • Bank statements (last 3 months) / proof of funds
  • Covering letter / invitation (for business or family visas)
  • Employment proof (if applicable)
  • Copy of sponsor’s ID / residence permit (for sponsored visas)

Fees & Processing Time (Indicative)

Fees: Visa fees vary by visa type, duration and service provider. Government fees + service/processing fees apply and they are non-refundable. Exact charge will be confirmed at the time of application.

Processing time (typical): tourist/business eVisas — usually 2–7 business days; work permits/residence — several weeks depending on sponsor & clearances. Emergency or expedited services may be available at additional cost.

Important Tips & Conditions

  • Make sure your passport has sufficient blank pages and 6+ months validity.
  • Check vaccination requirements and any entry health rules (e.g., COVID or other public health advisories) before travel.
  • Do not book non-refundable tickets until visa approval (unless otherwise advised).
  • Visa rules can change — rely on the official eVisa portal or your authorised agent (Sky Way) for the latest update.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I work on a tourist eVisa?

A: No. Employment requires a work visa sponsored by a Bahraini employer.

Q: How long is a Bahrain tourist eVisa valid for?

A: Validity depends on visa class — commonly 14, 30 or 90 days; check your visa stamp/eVisa for exact validity and permitted stay length.

Q: Are visa fees refundable if my application is refused?

A: No — government and processing fees are typically non-refundable. Sky Way will advise on grounds of refusal and next steps.

Q: Is travel insurance required?

A: Travel insurance is highly recommended and may be required depending on the visa class or airline policy.
