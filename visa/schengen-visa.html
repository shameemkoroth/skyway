Schengen Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

A single visa that opens the door to 29 European countries. Whether it's the snow-capped Alps of Switzerland, the art of Italy, or the fashion of France, the Schengen Visa is your key. Skyway Tours & Travels specializes in handling the complex documentation and VFS appointment booking process, ensuring your application stands the best chance of approval.

🚀 New "Cascade" Rule (Easier 2-Year Visas!)

Great News for Indians: A new EU rule now allows eligible Indian citizens to get a 2-Year Multiple Entry Visa after lawfully using just two visas in the last three years. This can later be upgraded to a 5-Year Visa!
No more applying every single time you travel.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

The standard "Type C" Short-Stay visa allows you to travel between all member countries freely.

1. Tourist Visa (Type C)

  • Purpose: Tourism, Family Visit, or Business.
  • Validity: Allows stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period.
  • Processing Time: Officially 15 days, but we recommend applying 1 to 2 months in advance due to high demand for appointment slots.
  • Fees: Please contact us for the latest embassy fees and service charges.

⚠️ The "First Point of Entry" Rule

You must apply at the Consulate of the country where you will spend the maximum number of days. If the duration is equal between countries, you must apply at the country of first entry.
Example: 4 days in Paris + 3 days in Rome = Apply at French Embassy.

Essential Documents Required

Schengen applications are strictly reviewed. We ensure your dossier is perfect.

  • Passport: Valid for at least 3 months after your return date.
  • Financials:
    • Original Bank Statements (Last 3-6 months) with Bank Stamp & Signature.
    • ITR (Income Tax Returns) for the last 3 years.
    • Salary Slips (Last 3 months) or Business Registration Proof.
  • Travel Insurance: Mandatory coverage of minimum 30,000 USD/Euros equivalent covering all Schengen states.
  • Itinerary: Confirmed Return Flight & Hotel Bookings for the entire stay.

Common Questions

Can I visit London (UK) with a Schengen Visa?

No. The UK is not part of the Schengen area. You need a separate Standard Visitor Visa for the UK.

What happens if my visa is rejected?

Visa fees are non-refundable. Rejections often happen due to "Unclear Purpose of Visit" or "Insufficient Funds." This is why a professional cover letter and itinerary from an agency is crucial.

Which countries are in the Schengen Area?

As of 2024-25, there are 29 countries: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria (Air/Sea), Croatia, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania (Air/Sea), Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, and Switzerland.
