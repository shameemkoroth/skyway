Morocco Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Morocco is the gateway to Africa—a dizzying combination of ancient medinas, vast deserts, and rugged mountains. For Indian citizens, the process has become much easier with the introduction of the E-Visa. Skyway Tours & Travels assists you with the Access Maroc application to ensure your journey to the Maghreb is smooth and secure.

🚀 Good News: E-Visa Open for Indians!

Indian citizens can now apply for the Electronic Visa (E-Visa) completely online. You no longer need to visit the Embassy in New Delhi or send physical documents by courier.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Tourist E-Visa (Access Maroc)

There are two processing speeds available on the official portal:

  • Standard Processing: Takes 3-4 business days.
  • Express Processing: Takes 24 hours (Higher Fee).
  • Validity: Valid for 180 days from issue.
  • Stay Duration: Allows a stay of up to 30 days (Single Entry).

⚠️ Website Scam Alert

There are many fake "Morocco Visa" websites charging 3x the price. The ONLY official government portal is acces-maroc.ma. Skyway Tours applies directly through this official channel on your behalf.

Essential Documents Required

The application is digital, but document quality matters. We will format them for you:

  • Passport Scan: Bio-page valid for at least 90 days beyond your exit date.
  • Photograph: Recent passport-style photo (35x45mm, White background).
  • Residency/Visa Proof (Optional but Helpful): If you hold a valid visa/residency for the US, UK, Schengen, Australia, or New Zealand, approval is often faster.
  • Standard Applicants: If you don't have a Western visa, you may need to provide a Bank Statement or Hotel Voucher.

Step-by-Step Application Process

  1. Share Details: Send your passport copy and photo to us via WhatsApp.
  2. Portal Entry: We create your account on Access Maroc and fill in the Arabic/French transliterated data required.
  3. Fee Payment: We pay the international government fee using our corporate card.
  4. Receive Visa: You get the E-Visa PDF by email. Print it out to show at Casablanca or Marrakesh airport.

🕌 Magical Things to Do in Morocco

From the Atlantic coast to the Sahara dunes, here is what you shouldn't miss:

Marrakesh Medina

Get lost in the souks of the Red City. Visit Jemaa el-Fnaa square at night for street food, snake charmers, and storytellers.

Chefchaouen (The Blue City)

Nestled in the Rif Mountains, this town is famous for its blue-washed buildings. It's a photographer's dream and very relaxed.

Sahara Desert (Merzouga)

Ride a camel into the sunset at Erg Chebbi dunes. Spend the night in a luxury Berber tent under a blanket of stars.

Common Questions

Is there a Visa on Arrival for Indians?

No. Morocco does NOT offer Visa on Arrival for Indian citizens. You must have an approved E-Visa or Sticker Visa before boarding your flight.

How long does the E-Visa take?

Standard processing is usually 3-4 days. However, delays happen. We recommend applying at least 10 days before travel.

Do I need Yellow Fever vaccination?

Generally No, unless you are arriving from a country with risk of Yellow Fever transmission (like some Sub-Saharan African nations). Direct flights from the Middle East/Europe do not require it.
