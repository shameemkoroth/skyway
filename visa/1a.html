Key Visa Categories for Indian Nationals

Bahrain offers several visa options depending on your purpose of travel. Most short-term visas are processed through the official Bahrain eVisa system.

1. Tourist / Visitor eVisa (Short Stay)

  • Purpose: Leisure, sightseeing, visiting friends/family, or attending non-business related events.
  • Validity: Common options include 7-day, 14-day, or 30-day stays. They can be single-entry or multi-entry.
  • Application: Processed online through the Bahrain eVisa portal.

2. Business Visa (Short Stay)

  • Purpose: Attending meetings, conferences, training, or short-term business negotiations.
  • Requirement: Requires a letter of invitation from the host company in Bahrain and may be processed as an eVisa or through the host's sponsorship.

3. Work / Employment Visa (Long Stay)

  • Purpose: Employment and long-term residence in Bahrain.
  • Process: Requires mandatory sponsorship by a Bahraini employer. The employer is responsible for securing the initial work permit and entry visa.
  • Note: This visa converts into a Residence Permit after arrival and medical checks.

Essential Documents Required (General Checklist)

Regardless of the visa type, the following documents are typically mandatory. Skyway Tours will provide a customised checklist based on your specific visa category.

A. Mandatory Base Documents

  • Valid Passport: Clear, coloured scan of the bio-page. Must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry to Bahrain.
  • Recent Photograph: One digital passport-size photo (usually 4x6 cm) meeting Bahraini visa photo specifications (white background, clear focus).
  • Completed Visa Application Form: Filled out accurately and signed.
  • Confirmed Travel: Copy of confirmed return/onward flight tickets.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel booking for the entire duration of the stay, or a letter from your sponsor/host in Bahrain with their full address.

B. Financial & Supporting Documents (Crucial for eVisa)

  • Proof of Funds: Scanned copies of your bank statements (for the last 3 to 6 months) showing sufficient funds to cover your stay in Bahrain.
  • Covering Letter: A personal letter from the applicant stating the purpose of the trip, dates of travel, and confirming financial responsibility.
  • Sponsorship Documents (if applicable): Invitation letter, and a copy of the sponsor’s Bahrain ID card (CPR) and resident permit.
  • Professional Proof: Letter from your current employer, salary slips, or business registration documents if self-employed.

Indicative Fees & Processing Times

The total cost for your Bahrain visa application will include the Government Visa Fee (varies by type/duration) plus the Skyway Tours Service Fee for documentation, application, and follow-up.

  • Tourist / Business eVisa:
    • Processing: Typically takes 3 to 7 working days after submission, but can be longer depending on government security checks.
    • Cost: Varies significantly. We will provide a precise quote upon review of your documents.
  • Work/Residence Visas: Processing times are longer, often 3–6 weeks, as they depend on the Bahraini employer and Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA) clearances.

Recommendation: We strongly advise initiating the application process at least 2–3 weeks before your intended travel date for eVisas to account for any unexpected delays.

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Do Not Overstay: Overstaying your visa validity can result in fines, detention, and bans on future entry into Bahrain or other GCC countries.
  • Travel Insurance: While not always mandatory, robust travel insurance covering medical emergencies is highly recommended.
  • Visa Policy Changes: Bahrain’s visa regulations can change frequently. Always consult with an authorized agent like Skyway Tours for the most current information before making travel arrangements.

Common Questions

Is a Visa-on-Arrival (VOA) an option for Indian citizens?

VOA is possible only for Indian nationals who hold a valid, unused visa/residence permit from specific countries (e.g., GCC, UK, US, Schengen). For all other cases, an eVisa is required prior to travel.

How do I know which eVisa duration to apply for?

You must apply for a visa that covers the duration of your confirmed flight and hotel bookings. We will confirm the appropriate visa class during our initial consultation.

What if my visa application is refused?

Government visa fees are typically non-refundable. Skyway Tours will analyse the refusal reason and advise you on the possibility of re-applying or appealing the decision.

Angola Visa Services: Your Gateway to Central Africa

Discover Angola with Skyway Tours & Travels, your partner for secure and efficient visa processing from India. Below is a clear summary of common Angolan visa types and major requirements.

Primary Visa Categories for Travel to Angola

1. Tourist Visa (Short-Term Stay)

  • Purpose: Tourism, leisure, short cultural visits, or visiting friends/family.
  • Validity: Usually up to 30 days; typically single-entry.
  • Requirement: Proof of funds and accommodation; sometimes an invitation letter.

2. Business Visa (Short-Term Stay)

  • Purpose: Business meetings, market visits, contract negotiations.
  • Validity: Often up to 30 days, single or multiple entry depending on the case.
  • Requirement: Notarized invitation from a registered Angolan company and host documentation.

3. Ordinary Visa (Long-Term Stay)

  • Purpose: Longer stays (assessment, investment, employment prospects); may be renewable.
  • Requirement: Detailed justification, supporting documents and possible legalisation/notarisation.

Mandatory Documentation for Angolan Visa Applications

  • Passport: Valid for at least 9 months beyond intended stay and with blank pages.
  • Photographs: Two recent passport-size photos (white background).
  • Visa Form: Completed, signed and, where required, notarised.
  • Yellow Fever Vaccination: International Health Certificate (original) where applicable.
  • Police Clearance Certificate: Original PCC (if requested) issued recently.

Processing time: Typically 10–15 working days, but can be longer due to legalization steps. Start at least 6–8 weeks prior to travel for complex cases.
