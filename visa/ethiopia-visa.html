Ethiopia Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Ethiopia, the Land of Origins, offers a unique blend of ancient history and stunning landscapes, from the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela to the bustling streets of Addis Ababa. Unlike many other African nations, Ethiopia has a streamlined E-Visa system for Indian citizens. Skyway Tours & Travels helps you navigate the online application process to ensure your visa is approved without errors.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

For most Indian travellers, the process is digital. However, specific health documentation is required alongside your visa.

1. Tourist E-Visa (Recommended)

  • Who is this for? For Indian citizens traveling for tourism, sightseeing, or visiting friends/relatives.
  • Process: Fully online. You do not need to submit your physical passport to an embassy. We handle the data entry and document upload for you.
  • Validity: Options for 30 Days (Single Entry) or 90 Days (Single Entry).
  • Entry Point: E-Visas are strictly valid only for arrival at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport.

2. Visa on Arrival (VOA)

  • Status: While technically available at Addis Ababa airport for Indians, it is not recommended due to long queues, potential system down-times, and stricter scrutiny.
  • Advisory: Immigration authorities strongly advise obtaining the E-Visa before departure to guarantee entry.

3. Business E-Visa

  • Requirement: Requires an invitation letter from the inviting company in Ethiopia and their Tax Identification Number (TIN) / Business License.

Essential Documents Required (E-Visa)

To process your E-Visa through Skyway Tours, we require digital copies of the following:

Mandatory Documents

  • Passport Scan: Bio-page of the passport. Must be valid for at least 6 months from the intended date of entry.
  • Photograph: A recent passport-size photo (White background, no glasses, clear face). We will resize it to meet the specific pixel requirements of the immigration portal.

Supporting Documents (Recommended)

  • Flight Tickets: Return or onward flight bookings.
  • Accommodation: Hotel booking details or host address in Ethiopia.

Processing Information

  • Processing Time: Standard processing takes 3 working days. However, we recommend applying at least 7 days in advance.
  • Delivery: The visa is issued as a PDF document which we will email to you. You must print this and carry it with you.

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Yellow Fever (CRITICAL): A valid Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate is MANDATORY for entry into Ethiopia. You may be asked to present this card at immigration. You must get vaccinated at least 10 days before travel.
  • Polio Vaccination: Indian citizens are often required to hold a valid Oral Polio Vaccination (OPV) certificate due to WHO regulations. Please carry your "Yellow Book" with this entry.
  • Currency:
    • The local currency is the Ethiopian Birr (ETB).
    • You must declare foreign currency exceeding $3,000 USD upon arrival. USD is the best currency to carry for exchange.
  • Altitude: Addis Ababa is located at a high altitude (2,355m). Travellers with heart or respiratory conditions should consult a doctor.

Common Questions

Can I enter Ethiopia by land with an E-Visa?

No. The E-Visa is currently valid only for arrival by air at Addis Ababa Bole International Airport.

Is the visa fee refundable if rejected?

No, the Ethiopian Immigration Department does not refund fees once the application is processed.

Do I need a transit visa if just changing planes in Addis Ababa?

If you are remaining within the airport transit area for a connecting flight (usually less than 12 hours) and not passing through immigration, you generally do not need a visa. If you plan to exit the airport, a Transit Visa or Tourist Visa is required.
