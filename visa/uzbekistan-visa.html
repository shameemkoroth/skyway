Uzbekistan Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Uzbekistan, the heart of the ancient Silk Road, is rapidly becoming a favorite destination for Indian tourists. With its turquoise-domed mosques, historic cities, and direct flights to Tashkent, the journey is easier than ever. Skyway Tours & Travels helps you navigate the simple E-Visa process and understands the critical local rules you must follow.

⚠️ Crucial Rule: Registration Slips

In Uzbekistan, you MUST get a registration slip from every hotel/hostel you stay at. These small paper slips are often checked by immigration officers when you exit the country. Do not lose them until you fly back home.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Electronic Visa (E-Visa)

The standard tourist visa for Indian citizens. It eliminates the need to visit the embassy.

  • Validity: Single or Double Entry options available. Usually valid for 90 days from issue, with a stay of up to 30 days.
  • Processing Time: Typically 3 to 4 working days.
  • Requirements: Clear passport scan and a digital photo.

2. 5-Day Visa-Free Transit

Indian citizens can enter Uzbekistan without a visa for up to 5 days IF they are transiting to a third country.

  • Condition: You must have a confirmed flight ticket to a third country (e.g., India -> Tashkent -> Russia/Europe).
  • Return Restriction: You cannot use this if you are flying back to the country you came from (e.g., India -> Tashkent -> India is NOT allowed).

3. Sticker Visa

Required only if you do not qualify for the E-Visa or need a long-term business/student visa. Requires a Letter of Invitation (LOI) approved by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Essential Documents Required (E-Visa)

We make the online application seamless for you. You need to provide:

  • Passport Scan: Front and back pages (Valid for at least 6 months).
  • Photograph: Digital photo with white background (35x45mm).
  • Travel Dates: Tentative entry and exit dates.
  • Home Address: Current residential address in India.

🇺🇿 The Jewel of the Silk Road: Main Attractions

Uzbekistan is best experienced by visiting its "Golden Triangle" cities connected by the high-speed Afrosiyob train:

Samarkand - The Registan

The crown jewel of the Silk Road. Visit the magnificent Registan Square, framed by three massive madrasas covered in intricate blue tiles.

Bukhara - The Living Museum

Walk through the ancient streets of the Old City. Don't miss the Kalyan Minaret, the Ark Fortress, and the trading domes.

Khiva - Itchan Kala

A walled open-air museum city. Step back in time inside the fortress walls (Itchan Kala) where cars are banned and history lives.

Tashkent - Modern Capital

A mix of Soviet architecture and modern parks. Visit the Chorsu Bazaar for spices and dry fruits, and see the world's oldest Quran at Hazrati Imam.

Amir Timur Mausoleum

Located in Samarkand, this is the final resting place of the great conqueror Tamerlane. The interior gold leaf decoration is breathtaking.

Uzbek Plov Centers

Food is a major attraction! Visit the massive "Plov Centers" in Tashkent or Samarkand to try the national rice dish cooked in giant cauldrons.

Common Questions

Can I get a Visa on Arrival?

Generally, No. Visa on Arrival is only for citizens of countries without Uzbek embassies or with specific government approval. Indians should strictly stick to the E-Visa.

Is it safe for solo female travellers?

Yes, Uzbekistan is considered one of the safest countries in the world for tourists, with very low crime rates and helpful "Tourist Police" in major cities.

What currency should I take?

Carry US Dollars. Ensure the notes are new, crisp, and have no markings or tears, or they will not be accepted for exchange.
