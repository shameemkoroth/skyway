Visa Types for Indian Nationals

Indian citizens have a straightforward digital entry process. Choosing the right validity period is key to a smooth trip.

1. Visitor E-Visa (Short Term)

  • Purpose: Tourism, visiting family, or short medical visits.
  • Validity: Valid for 90 days from the date of issue.
  • Stay Duration: Allows a stay of up to 21 days in the country.
  • Entry Type: Single Entry.

2. Visitor E-Visa (Long Term)

  • Purpose: Extended holidays or longer family visits.
  • Validity: Valid for 180 days from the date of issue.
  • Stay Duration: Allows a stay of up to 120 days in the country.

3. Visa on Arrival (Conditional)

  • Important: Visa on Arrival is NOT available to all Indians. You are only eligible if you hold a valid permanent residence or a valid visa from the USA, UK, Schengen countries, GCC countries, etc.
  • Recommendation: We strongly advise applying for the E-Visa in advance to avoid deportation risk if your documents are not accepted at the border.

Essential Documents Required

The documentation for an Armenia E-Visa is fully digital. Skyway Tours ensures your scans meet the Ministry of Foreign Affairs specifications.

Mandatory Documents

  • Passport Scan: A clear, coloured scan of your passport bio-page. The passport must be valid for at least 3 to 6 months beyond your intended stay.
  • Photograph: A recent digital passport-sized photo (white background) with no glasses.

Supporting Documents (Immigration)

While the E-Visa is easy to get, immigration officers at Zvartnots International Airport (EVN) are strict. You must carry:

  • Flight Tickets: Confirmed return tickets are mandatory.
  • Accommodation Proof: Hotel booking vouchers or a registered invitation address.
  • Medical Insurance: HIGHLY RECOMMENDED: While not always checked for the visa *application*, it is legally required for entry. Medical treatment for foreigners is not free.
  • Sufficient Funds: Proof of funds (Cash/Card) to cover your stay.

Common Questions

Can I extend my E-Visa?

Yes, visitor visas can usually be extended for up to 60 days by applying at the Migration and Citizenship Service in Yerevan, but it is a complex process. It is better to apply for the 120-day visa initially if you plan a long stay.

Do I need a transit visa?

If you stay in the transit zone of the airport and do not pass through immigration, no visa is required. If you wish to enter the city during a layover, you need a valid E-Visa.

Is the E-Visa valid for land borders?

Yes, the E-Visa is valid for entry via the land borders with Georgia (Bagratashen, Bavra, Gogavan).
