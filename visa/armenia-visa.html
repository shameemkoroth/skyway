Armenia Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Nestled in the Caucasus mountains, Armenia offers a unique blend of ancient Christian heritage, stunning alpine landscapes, and the cosmopolitan vibe of Yerevan. Skyway Tours & Travels makes your journey simple by handling your E-Visa processing and ensuring you meet all new 2025 entry requirements.

⚠️ Do you qualify for Visa on Arrival?

Standard Indian tourists CANNOT get a Visa on Arrival. You must apply for an E-Visa before flying.
Exception: You are eligible for Visa on Arrival ONLY IF you hold a valid visa or residence permit from the USA, UK, Schengen, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Kuwait, or Oman.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Electronic Visa (E-Visa)

This is the standard requirement for most Indian tourists. It is applied for online and received via email.

  • Short Term: Valid for 21 days (Stay duration).
  • Long Term: Valid for 120 days (Stay duration).
  • Processing Time: Typically 3 to 4 working days.
  • Requirement: No physical submission of passport is needed.

2. Sticker Visa

Required if you do not qualify for an E-Visa or are traveling for specific long-term purposes (Work/Student) that require embassy interaction.

Essential Documents Required (E-Visa)

We ensure your application is error-free to avoid rejection. You will need:

  • Passport Scan: Valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.
  • Photograph: Recent digital photo with white background.
  • Flight & Hotel: Confirmed return tickets and hotel accommodation proof.
  • Bank Statements: Proof of sufficient funds (last 3 months) is highly recommended.
  • Medical Insurance: Mandatory. You must have valid travel health insurance covering your stay in Armenia.

🇦🇲 Land of Noah: Main Attractions

From the view of Mount Ararat to the world's longest cable car, Armenia is breathtaking:

Yerevan - The Pink City

Explore the Cascades Art Complex, Republic Square with its singing fountains, and the vibrant cafes of Northern Avenue.

Lake Sevan

One of the largest high-altitude lakes in the world. Visit the Sevanavank Monastery on the peninsula for a stunning panoramic view.

Tatev Monastery & Cable Car

Ride the "Wings of Tatev," the world's longest non-stop double track cable car, soaring over a gorge to reach the ancient monastery.

Garni Temple

The only standing Greco-Roman colonnaded building in Armenia. A pagan temple from the 1st century AD set against a dramatic gorge.

Geghard Monastery

A UNESCO World Heritage site partially carved out of the adjacent mountain, surrounded by cliffs at the entrance to the Azat Valley.

Dilijan

Known as the "Switzerland of Armenia." A lush, forested town famous for its spa resorts, traditional craft workshops, and hiking trails.

Common Questions

Is Travel Insurance mandatory?

Yes. You may be asked to show proof of medical insurance at the border control. Without it, you may be required to buy it at the airport or face delays.

Can I enter via land border from Georgia?

Yes, the E-Visa is valid for entry via land borders (Bagratashen/Sadakhlo) if you are traveling by train or car from Tbilisi.

What currency should I carry?

The currency is the **Armenian Dram (AMD)**. It is best to carry USD or Euros to exchange in Yerevan city center for the best rates.
