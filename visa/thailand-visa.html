Thailand Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

From the bustling street markets of Bangkok to the turquoise waters of Koh Phi Phi, Thailand is the most popular holiday destination for Indian families. Skyway Tours & Travels brings you the latest updates: Indian citizens can currently enjoy Visa-Free Entry for longer durations! However, new digital registration rules are being implemented.

🎉 60-Day Visa Exemption (Visa Free!)

Great News: Indian passport holders can now enter Thailand Visa-Free for a stay of up to 60 Days! This is perfect for long holidays.
Note: This replaces the older 15/30 day rules for most tourists.

Entry Requirements for Indian Nationals

1. Visa Exemption (Recommended)

Simply fly to Thailand and get stamped in for 60 days. No prior visa application is needed.

  • Stay Duration: Up to 60 Days.
  • Extension: Can be extended for another 30 days at a local immigration office.
  • Cost: Free at the airport.

2. Visa on Arrival (VoA)

If you prefer the old method or are not eligible for exemption (rare for Indians now), VoA is still available but grants only 15 Days.

  • Queue: Often has long waiting lines at Suvarnabhumi/Don Mueang airports.
  • Requirement: Must show confirmed return ticket within 15 days.

⚠️ Mandatory: Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)

Thailand is rolling out the Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) / TDAC system. All visa-exempt travellers will soon be required to register online before flying to receive a QR code for entry.
Skyway Tours can assist you with this digital registration to ensure you have the correct QR code before departure.

Essential Documents Required

Even with Visa-Free entry, Immigration officers may check:

  • Passport: Valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry.
  • Return Ticket: Confirmed return flight within the 60-day limit.
  • Accommodation: Proof of hotel booking or host address.
  • Funds: Proof of sufficient financial means (Cash or Bank Balance) to cover your stay.

🏝️ Land of Smiles: Top Things to Do

Thailand offers everything from spirituality to wild nightlife. Here are the top picks:

The Grand Palace (Bangkok)

The spectacular official residence of the Kings of Siam. Marvel at the intricate architecture and the Temple of the Emerald Buddha (Wat Phra Kaew).

Phi Phi Islands

Famous for Maya Bay (from the movie The Beach). Crystal clear waters, limestone cliffs, and vibrant marine life make it a snorkeling paradise.

Chiang Mai Elephant Sanctuaries

Visit an ethical sanctuary in the northern mountains. Feed, bathe, and walk with rescued elephants in their natural habitat (No riding!).

Ayutthaya

A UNESCO World Heritage site just north of Bangkok. Explore the ancient ruins of the second capital of the Siamese Kingdom.

Sanctuary of Truth (Pattaya)

An awe-inspiring wooden structure filled with sculptures based on traditional Buddhist and Hindu motifs. It is entirely made of wood without metal nails.

Railay Beach (Krabi)

Accessible only by boat due to high limestone cliffs cutting it off from the mainland. A world-class destination for rock climbing and sunsets.

Common Questions

Is the 60-day entry guaranteed?

It is granted at the discretion of the immigration officer. Having a return ticket and proof of funds makes it nearly guaranteed.

Can I convert Visa Exemption to a Work Visa?

Yes, it is possible to convert a Tourist entry into a Non-Immigrant B (Work) visa inside Thailand, but it requires extensive paperwork from your employer.

Do I need Yellow Fever vaccination?

Indian citizens generally do not need Yellow Fever vaccination for Thailand unless arriving from a Yellow Fever endemic country (like parts of Africa/South America).
