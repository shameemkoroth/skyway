Turkey Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Straddling two continents, Turkey is a mesmerizing blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. From the balloons of Cappadocia to the bustling bazaars of Istanbul, it is a top destination for Indian tourists. However, choosing the right visa is critical. Skyway Tours & Travels assists you with both E-Visas and Sticker Visas, ensuring a hassle-free journey.

⚠️ Important: Do you qualify for an E-Visa?

Not all Indian citizens are eligible for the quick E-Visa. You can ONLY apply for an E-Visa if you hold a valid visa or residence permit from the USA, UK, Ireland, or Schengen countries. If you do not have one of these, you must apply for a Sticker Visa via the consulate.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Conditional E-Visa (Online)

The fastest way to enter Turkey, but strictly limited to specific document holders.

  • Eligibility: Must possess a valid visa/residency from US, UK, Ireland, or Schengen. (E-visas of other countries are generally not accepted as supporting documents).
  • Processing Time: Almost instant (usually within 24 hours).
  • Validity: Single Entry, valid for 180 days (Stay up to 30 days).
  • Requirement: Passport, Supporting Visa info, and Flight details.

2. Standard Sticker Visa

For Indian tourists who do not hold US/UK/Schengen visas. This requires physical submission of documents.

  • Process: Documents are submitted via the authorized visa application center. Biometrics may be required.
  • Processing Time: Typically takes 15 to 20 working days. Apply well in advance!
  • Validity: Usually Single Entry (Stay duration decided by the Embassy).

Essential Documents Required (Sticker Visa)

The documentation for the Sticker Visa is strict. We assist you in organizing:

  • Original Passport: Valid for at least 6 months from travel date with 2 blank pages.
  • Photographs: 2 recent photos (2.5 inch x 2.5 inch, white background, matte finish).
  • Bank Statements: Last 6 months original updated bank statement with sufficient balance and bank seal.
  • Employment Proof: Salary slips (last 3 months) and Leave Authorization Letter (NOC) from employer.
  • Travel Insurance: Mandatory travel medical insurance (We provide this).
  • Confirmed Bookings: Flight reservation and Hotel accommodation details.

🇹🇷 The Crossroads of Civilization: Main Attractions

Turkey offers diverse experiences ranging from ancient history to stunning coastlines. Here are the top things to do:

Istanbul - Hagia Sophia & Blue Mosque

Explore the historic Sultanahmet district. Marvel at the Hagia Sophia's dome, the Blue Mosque's tiles, and shop at the Grand Bazaar.

Cappadocia - Hot Air Balloons

Witness the sunrise over the surreal "Fairy Chimneys" from a hot air balloon. Stay in a unique cave hotel for the full experience.

Pamukkale - The Cotton Castle

Walk barefoot on the gleaming white travertine terraces filled with thermal blue waters, a natural wonder and ancient spa center.

Ephesus Ancient City

Walk through one of the best-preserved Roman cities in the Mediterranean. See the Library of Celsus and the Great Theatre.

Antalya - The Turquoise Coast

Relax on stunning Mediterranean beaches, explore the waterfalls, or wander through Kaleiçi (Old Town) with its Ottoman-era houses.

Bosphorus Cruise

Take a boat ride along the strait that separates Europe and Asia. Enjoy views of palaces, fortresses, and bridges connecting the continents.

Common Questions

Can I get a Visa on Arrival in Turkey?

No. Indian citizens are not eligible for Visa on Arrival. You must obtain an E-Visa or Sticker Visa before departure.

Is travel insurance mandatory?

Yes. You cannot apply for a Turkey visa without valid travel medical insurance covering your dates of travel.

What if my US/UK visa is expired?

If your supporting US/UK/Schengen visa has expired, you are not eligible for the E-Visa. You must apply for the standard Sticker Visa.
