UAE (Dubai) Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

From the futuristic skyline of Dubai to the cultural richness of Abu Dhabi, the United Arab Emirates remains the top destination for Indian tourists. Skyway Tours & Travels simplifies your entry with fast processing for 30-day and 60-day visas, ensuring you meet all new 2025 compliance requirements.

⚠️ 2025 Rule Update: No Grace Period

The UAE has officially abolished the 10-day grace period for tourist visas. You must exit the country on or before the last day of your visa validity. Overstaying attracts heavy daily fines and potential travel bans.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Pre-Arranged Tourist Visa (Standard)

This is the most common visa for Indian families and tourists. It is an electronic visa (E-Visa) that we process before you fly.

  • 30 Days Visa: Single or Multiple Entry. Best for short holidays.
  • 60 Days Visa: Single or Multiple Entry. Best for job seekers or long family visits.
  • Processing Time: Typically 1 to 2 working days.
  • Insurance: Mandatory medical insurance is now required and is included in our package.

2. Visa on Arrival (Conditional)

You can get a visa directly at Dubai/Abu Dhabi airport ONLY IF you meet specific criteria:

  • You hold a valid US Visa or Green Card (valid for at least 6 months).
  • You hold a valid Residence Visa from UK or EU countries.
  • Validity: 14 Days (Extendable once for a fee).

3. Transit Visa (48 or 96 Hours)

Strictly for travellers with a connecting flight to a third country.

  • 48 Hours: Free of charge (airline handling fees apply).
  • 96 Hours: Paid visa for longer stopovers.
  • Requirement: Confirmed ticket to a third destination (not back to India).

Essential Documents Required

For the Standard Tourist Visa application, we need soft copies of:

  • Passport: Front and back pages (Valid for at least 6 months).
  • Photograph: Passport-size photo with white background.
  • Pan Card: Copy of applicant's Pan Card (Required for payment/TCS compliance).
  • Supporting Docs: Confirmed return flight tickets are highly recommended before visa application.

🇦🇪 The Emirates Experience: Main Attractions

The UAE is more than just shopping malls. Discover these world-class attractions:

Burj Khalifa & Downtown Dubai

Visit the observation deck of the world's tallest building. Watch the Dubai Fountain show and shop at the massive Dubai Mall.

Museum of the Future

Dubai's newest icon. An architectural marvel exploring the future of science, technology, and innovation. Book tickets weeks in advance!

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque (Abu Dhabi)

One of the world's largest mosques. Stunning white marble, massive chandeliers, and reflective pools make it a photographer's dream.

Ferrari World & Yas Island

Home to the world's fastest rollercoaster, Formula Rossa. Yas Island also hosts Warner Bros. World and Yas Waterworld.

Global Village

A massive multicultural festival park combining culture, shopping, and entertainment from 90 countries. (Open seasonally: Oct to April).

Desert Safari

No trip is complete without dune bashing in a 4x4, camel riding, and a BBQ dinner under the stars at a Bedouin camp.

Common Questions

Is travel insurance mandatory for UAE?

Yes. Tourists are required to have medical insurance covering their stay. Skyway Tours includes this in your visa package.

Can I convert my Tourist Visa to a Work Visa?

Yes, you can find a job on a tourist visa and change your status to a residency visa inside the country without exiting (subject to fees).

What happens if I overstay?

Since the removal of the grace period, you will be fined from Day 1 of overstay. You may also require an "Outpass" to leave the country if fines are high.
