Vietnam Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Vietnam has exploded in popularity among Indian tourists, offering everything from the limestone pillars of Halong Bay to the lantern-lit streets of Hoi An. Skyway Tours & Travels helps you navigate the 90-day E-Visa process effortlessly, ensuring you avoid common mistakes that lead to denied entry.

⚠️ Pro Tip: Avoid Name Mismatches!

The #1 reason for Vietnam E-Visa refusal or denied boarding is a name mismatch. Your E-Visa name must match the Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) at the bottom of your passport exactly. We ensure your application is 100% compliant before submission.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Electronic Visa (E-Visa)

Since late 2023, Vietnam has granted E-Visas to all nationalities, including Indians, with extended validity.

  • Validity: Up to 90 Days.
  • Entry Type: Choose between Single Entry or Multiple Entry.
  • Processing Time: Typically 3 to 5 working days (We recommend applying 2 weeks in advance).
  • Entry Ports: Valid at 33 international checkpoints including all major airports (Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh, Da Nang) and land borders.

2. Phu Quoc Visa Exemption (30 Days)

Indian citizens can enter Phu Quoc Island Visa-Free for up to 30 days under strict conditions:

  • You must fly directly to Phu Quoc from an international country (e.g., India -> Thailand -> Phu Quoc).
  • OR transit via a Vietnamese international airport (like Hanoi/HCM) without leaving the transit area.
  • Restriction: You CANNOT visit other parts of Vietnam (like Ho Chi Minh City) with this exemption. If you plan to travel to the mainland, you MUST have an E-Visa.

3. Visa on Arrival (VoA)

Requires a pre-approved "Visa Approval Letter." This is generally more cumbersome than the E-Visa and is mostly used for urgent cases or business travel.

Essential Documents Required (E-Visa)

To ensure a smooth approval, we require:

  • Passport Scan: Clear scan of the bio-page (Valid for at least 6 months).
  • Photograph: Digital photo (4x6 cm ratio) with white background, without glasses.
  • Intended Dates: Arrival and Departure dates (The visa validity starts from the date you select).
  • Entry/Exit Port: You must specify exactly which airport/border you will enter through.

🇻🇳 Land of the Blue Dragon: Main Attractions

Vietnam is a long, narrow country with distinct climates and vibes. Here are the top spots to include in your itinerary:

Halong Bay & Lan Ha Bay

A UNESCO World Heritage site. Take an overnight cruise to sail among thousands of limestone karsts rising from emerald waters.

Da Nang & The Golden Bridge

Walk on the famous "Hands of God" bridge at Ba Na Hills. Da Nang also offers pristine beaches like My Khe and the Dragon Bridge.

Hoi An Ancient Town

A charming yellow-walled town famous for its colorful lanterns, tailor shops, and night markets. Best explored by bicycle.

Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon)

The bustling southern metropolis. Visit the War Remnants Museum, the Cu Chi Tunnels, and the French colonial Post Office.

Ninh Binh

Known as "Halong Bay on Land." Take a rowboat tour through caves and rice paddies in Tam Coc or Trang An.

Sapa

In the northern mountains, Sapa is famous for its trekking trails, cascading rice terraces, and diverse hill-tribe culture.

Common Questions

Do I need to show proof of funds?

While not always asked, Immigration officers may request to see return tickets and proof of accommodation (hotel booking) or sufficient cash (USD/VND).

Can I enter Vietnam before the visa start date?

No. You can enter on or after the "Valid From" date on your E-Visa, but never before. You can leave anytime before the "Valid Until" date.

Do I need to register with the police?

Hotels will do this for you. However, if you stay in an Airbnb or homestay, ensure your host registers your temporary residence with the local police within 24 hours to avoid fines.
