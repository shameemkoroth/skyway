Cambodia Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

From the magnificent spires of Angkor Wat to the untouched beaches of Koh Rong, Cambodia is the "Kingdom of Wonder." For Indian citizens, entry is convenient, but new 2025 digital regulations mean you must be prepared before you fly. Skyway Tours & Travels assists with E-Visas and the new mandatory arrival declarations.

⚠️ New 2025 Rule: E-Arrival Card (CeA)

Cambodia has abolished paper immigration forms. All travellers (Visa on Arrival OR E-Visa holders) MUST complete the "Cambodia E-Arrival (CeA)" digital declaration 7 days before arrival. You must present the generated QR code at immigration.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Tourist E-Visa (Type T) - Recommended

The most hassle-free option. Apply online and skip the visa queue at the airport.

  • Validity: 3 Months from issue date.
  • Stay Duration: 30 Days (Single Entry).
  • Processing Time: 3 to 5 Business Days.
  • Entry Points: Valid at major airports (Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Sihanoukville) and main land borders.

2. Visa on Arrival (VoA)

Indian citizens can obtain a visa upon landing, though queues can be long.

  • Requirements: Valid passport, return ticket, and sufficient cash for the visa fee.
  • Note: You still need to complete the E-Arrival Card (CeA) beforehand.

Essential Documents Required (E-Visa)

For a smooth E-Visa application via Skyway Tours, we need:

  • Passport Scan: Bio-page valid for at least 6 months beyond travel date.
  • Photograph: Recent digital photo (White background, JPEG format).
  • Travel Details: Intended date of entry and point of entry (Airport/Border).
  • Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation (required for E-Arrival Card).

🇰🇭 Kingdom of Wonder: Main Attractions

Cambodia offers a mix of ancient history, dark tourism, and tropical relaxation:

Angkor Wat (Siem Reap)

The world's largest religious monument. Wake up early for the iconic sunrise over the lotus towers reflecting in the pond.

Ta Prohm (Tomb Raider Temple)

Famous for the massive silk-cotton tree roots strangling the ancient ruins, creating an atmospheric jungle temple experience.

Phnom Penh Royal Palace

A gleaming complex of Khmer roofs and gold. Visit the Silver Pagoda, where the floor is tiled with 5,000 silver blocks.

Tuol Sleng & Killing Fields

A sobering look at Cambodia's tragic history under the Khmer Rouge. Essential for understanding the resilience of the local people.

Koh Rong & Koh Rong Sanloem

Pristine white-sand islands off the coast of Sihanoukville. Perfect for snorkeling, diving, and bioluminescent plankton tours.

Tonle Sap Floating Villages

Take a boat tour to see entire communities living on the water, complete with floating schools, markets, and basketball courts.

Common Questions

Can I pay the Visa on Arrival fee in Rupees?

No. You must pay in US Dollars (USD). Ensure you carry new, crisp notes as torn or old bills are often rejected.

Is the E-Arrival Card free?

Yes, the government E-Arrival Card submission itself is free, but it is a mandatory step separate from the paid Visa.

Can I extend my tourist visa?

Yes, the 30-day tourist visa (Type T) can be extended once for another 30 days at the Immigration Department in Phnom Penh.
