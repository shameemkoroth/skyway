Visa Types for Indian Nationals

Cambodia offers Indian citizens two main routes for entry. The E-Visa is generally recommended for peace of mind.

1. Tourist E-Visa (Type T) - Recommended

  • Purpose: Strictly for tourism and leisure.
  • Validity: The visa is valid for 3 months (90 days) from the date of approval.
  • Stay Duration: Allows a single stay of up to 30 days.
  • Entry Type: Single Entry only.
  • Entry Points: Valid at all major international airports (Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Sihanoukville) and major land borders (Poi Pet, Bavet, Cham Yeam, Tropaeng Kreal).

2. Visa on Arrival (VOA)

  • Eligibility: Indian citizens are eligible for VOA at international airports.
  • Requirements: You must carry a completed application form (provided on the flight), a recent photograph (4x6 cm), and the visa fee in USD Cash.
  • Drawback: Queues can be long and chaotic during peak tourist seasons. You also risk being scammed at land borders if you do not have a visa in advance.

Essential Documents Required

The E-Visa process is fully digital. Skyway Tours ensures your application is formatted correctly to avoid delays.

Mandatory Documents

  • Passport Scan: Clear scan of the passport bio-page. Must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry.
  • Photograph: A recent digital passport-sized photo (white background). Selfies or cropped photos are often rejected.

Supporting Documents (Immigration)

Upon arrival at the immigration counter, you should be prepared to show:

  • Return Flight Ticket: Confirmed ticket for your return or onward journey.
  • Hotel Booking: Proof of accommodation for your stay.
  • E-Visa Printout: You must print 2 copies of your E-Visa certificate (one for entry, one for exit).

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Currency: Cambodia uses a dual-currency system. The US Dollar (USD) is widely used and preferred for almost all transactions (hotels, tuk-tuks, restaurants). The local currency (Riel) is used for small change (less than $1). Ensure your USD notes are crisp and new; old or torn notes are often rejected.
  • Angkor Wat Pass: The entrance pass for the Angkor Archaeological Park is separate from your visa. It can be bought online or at the ticket center in Siem Reap.
  • Land Borders: If entering via bus from Thailand or Vietnam, beware of "visa service" scams at the border. Having an E-Visa in advance avoids these hassles.

Common Questions

Can I extend my Tourist Visa?

Yes, the 30-day tourist visa (Type T) can usually be extended once for an additional 30 days at the Immigration Department in Phnom Penh.

Do I need a Yellow Fever vaccination?

Generally, no. Yellow Fever vaccination is only required if you are traveling from a country with risk of Yellow Fever transmission (parts of Africa/South America). It is not required for direct travel from India.
