Bahrain Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Heading to the "Pearl of the Gulf"? Whether you are visiting Manama for its vibrant souqs, the Bahrain International Circuit for F1, or for short business meetings, Skyway Tours & Travels ensures your entry into the Kingdom is smooth. We specialize in processing the Bahrain E-Visa for Indian citizens, helping you navigate the strict financial and documentation requirements.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

For most Indian tourists, the E-Visa is the standard and safest method of entry. Visa on Arrival is highly restricted (see details below).

1. Tourist E-Visa (Standard Option)

  • Purpose: Tourism, visiting family, and business meetings.
  • Validity Options:
    • 2 Weeks (Single Entry): Valid for use within 30 days of approval. Stay up to 14 days.
    • 1 Month (Multiple Entry): Valid for 3 months. Stay up to 30 days per visit.
    • 1 Year (Multiple Entry): Valid for 1 year. Stay up to 90 days per visit.

2. Visa on Arrival (Conditional)

  • Warning: Ordinary Indian passport holders generally DO NOT qualify for Visa on Arrival unless they meet specific criteria.
  • Eligibility: You may be eligible for VOA only if:
    • You hold a valid permanent residence permit for a GCC country (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar) valid for at least 3 months.
    • You hold a valid, multi-entry visa for the USA, UK, or Schengen Area.
  • Recommendation: If you do not meet the above criteria, you must apply for an E-Visa before flying. Airlines will deny boarding otherwise.

Essential Documents Required

Bahraini authorities are strict about financial proof. Skyway Tours ensures your application dossier is complete to avoid rejection.

Mandatory Documents

  • Passport Scan: Clear scan of bio-pages. Must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival.
  • Photograph: Recent passport-sized digital photo (white background).
  • Bank Statements: CRITICAL: You must provide the last 3 months of bank statements. The closing balance must be approximately BHD 300 (approx. INR 65,000 - 70,000) or equivalent in USD. Applications with insufficient funds are frequently rejected.

Supporting Documents (Immigration)

Upon arrival at Bahrain International Airport (BAH), you must present:

  • Return Flight Ticket: Confirmed ticket for your return or onward journey.
  • Hotel Booking: Confirmed hotel voucher or details of your relative's address in Bahrain (Invitation letter may be required).
  • E-Visa Printout: Always carry a hard copy of your approved E-Visa.

Fees & Processing Times

We handle the online portal submission, resizing of documents, and foreign currency payments.

  • Processing Time: Typically takes 3 to 5 working days. Delays can occur during public holidays or if the immigration department requests additional documents.
  • Urgency: Apply at least 2 weeks before your travel date to be safe.

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Currency: The currency is the Bahraini Dinar (BHD). It is one of the highest-valued currencies in the world. Carry USD for easy exchange.
  • Health Requirements:
    • Yellow Fever: Mandatory only if arriving from a Yellow Fever endemic country (e.g., parts of Africa/South America). Direct travelers from India generally do not need this.
    • Medications: Bahrain has strict drug laws. Carry a doctor's prescription for any prescription medications you are carrying.
  • Customs: Strict penalties apply for bringing in banned substances. Alcohol allowance is limited for non-Muslims only.
