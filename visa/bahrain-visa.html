Bahrain Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Known as the "Pearl of the Gulf," Bahrain is a laid-back island nation perfect for a weekend getaway. It blends rich history with a modern lifestyle. Skyway Tours & Travels simplifies your entry process, whether you need an E-Visa or qualify for Visa on Arrival under specific conditions.

⚠️ Do you qualify for Visa on Arrival?

Indian citizens usually require a pre-approved E-Visa.
Exception: You are eligible for Visa on Arrival ONLY IF you hold a valid visa or residence permit for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, UK, USA, or Schengen countries (Valid for at least 3 months).

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Electronic Visa (E-Visa)

The standard tourist visa for Indians who do not hold other GCC/Western visas.

  • 14 Days Single Entry: Best for short holidays.
  • 1 Year Multiple Entry: Allows stays of up to 90 days per visit.
  • Processing Time: Typically 3 to 5 working days.
  • Requirement: Applied online before departure.

2. Visa on Arrival (Conditional)

If you meet the eligibility (holding US/UK/Schengen/GCC visa), you can get a visa directly at Bahrain International Airport.

  • Validity: Usually 14 days (Extendable inside Bahrain).
  • Fee: Paid at the immigration counter (Card/Cash).

Essential Documents Required

Whether applying for E-Visa or On-Arrival, you must have:

  • Passport: Valid for at least 6 months from the date of departure (not just entry).
  • Return Flight Ticket: Confirmed booking leaving Bahrain.
  • Proof of Funds: Bank statements (last 3 months) showing a minimum balance (approx 1000 USD equivalent is recommended).
  • Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation or relative's address details.

🇧🇭 The Pearl of the Gulf: Main Attractions

Bahrain is small but packed with culture and fun activities:

Qal'at al-Bahrain (Bahrain Fort)

A UNESCO World Heritage site. Explore the ancient ruins of the Dilmun civilization and enjoy a beautiful sunset over the fort.

Manama Souq & Bab Al Bahrain

Walk through the historic gateway into the bustling souq. Shop for pearls, spices, perfumes, and traditional sweets (Halwa).

Al Fateh Grand Mosque

One of the largest mosques in the world, capable of holding 7,000 worshippers. It features a massive fiberglass dome and exquisite calligraphy.

Tree of Life (Shajarat-al-Hayat)

A 400-year-old mesquite tree standing alone in the middle of the desert with no known water source. A mystical natural wonder.

Bahrain International Circuit

Home of the F1 Grand Prix. You can take a guided tour of the track or even book a driving experience on non-race days.

Jarada Island

A disappearing island! This white sand strip appears only at low tide. Accessible by boat for swimming in crystal clear waters.

Common Questions

Is travel insurance mandatory?

While not strictly enforced at the border like in Europe, it is highly recommended to have coverage for medical emergencies.

Can I drive in Bahrain with an Indian license?

You can drive on a valid Indian license for a short period (usually related to rental cars), but an International Driving Permit (IDP) is safer and recommended.

What is the currency?

The **Bahraini Dinar (BHD)**. It is a high-value currency. Carry USD or exchange currency at authorized centers in Manama.
