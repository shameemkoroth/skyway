Jordan Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

From the rose-red city of Petra to the Martian landscapes of Wadi Rum, Jordan is a bucket-list destination. For Indian citizens, entry is straightforward, but choosing the right visa method can save you thousands of rupees. Skyway Tours & Travels helps you navigate the Jordan Pass application and entry process to ensure a hassle-free arrival.

💡 The "Jordan Pass" Strategy (Highly Recommended)

Instead of paying for a visa separately, buy the Jordan Pass online before you travel. It acts as your Pre-Paid Visa (fee waived) AND your entry ticket to Petra, Wadi Rum, and 40+ other sites.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Visa on Arrival (VoA) - The "Jordan Pass" Route

This is the most popular and cost-effective method for tourists.

  • Requirement: You must purchase the Jordan Pass online before arrival.
  • Condition: You must stay in Jordan for a minimum of 3 consecutive nights (4 days).
  • Cost: Starts at 70 JOD (approx ₹8,200). This includes the visa fee waiver + entry to Petra (worth 50 JOD) + 40 other sites.
  • Benefit: You save approx 40 JOD (visa fee) + 50 JOD (Petra ticket) individually. Total savings ~₹9,000+.

2. Standard Visa on Arrival (Without Pass)

If you are staying less than 3 nights or not visiting Petra, you can pay directly at the airport.

  • Cost: 40 JOD (approx ₹4,700) payable in Cash/Card at the immigration counter.
  • Process: Queue at the "Visa Fee" counter first, pay the fee, get a receipt, then queue for immigration.
  • Validity: 30 Days (Single Entry).

3. E-Visa (Ministry of Interior)

You can apply online via the MOI website if you prefer having a visa approval in hand before flying, though VoA is standard for Indians.

Essential Documents Required

Whether you use the Jordan Pass or pay for VoA, immigration officers will check:

  • Original Passport: Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond travel date.
  • Return Flight Ticket: Confirmed booking back to India or onward destination.
  • Accommodation Proof: Hotel bookings for the duration of stay (Crucial to prove the 3-night requirement for Jordan Pass holders).
  • Financial Means: Approx $1000 USD equivalent in cash or cards to prove you can support your stay.

Step-by-Step Arrival Guide (Queen Alia Airport)

If you have the Jordan Pass:

  1. Skip the "Visa Fee Payment" queue.
  2. Go directly to the Immigration/Passport Control desk.
  3. Show your Jordan Pass (Printed or Mobile PDF) along with your passport.
  4. The officer will scan the QR code, verify your stay duration (return ticket), and stamp your passport Free of Charge.

If you are Paying Cash:

  1. Go to the "Visa on Arrival" bank counter first.
  2. Pay 40 JOD (Currency exchange is available nearby, but rates may be high. Carrying USD to exchange is best).
  3. Take the receipt to the Immigration desk to get your stamp.

Common Questions

What if I stay less than 3 nights with the Jordan Pass?

If you use the Jordan Pass to enter but leave before spending 3 nights, immigration will charge you the standard visa fee (40 JOD) upon exit at the airport.

Is Yellow Fever vaccination required?

Generally No for travellers arriving directly from India. However, if you are transiting through African countries (like Ethiopia/Kenya) for more than 12 hours, a Yellow Fever certificate is mandatory.

Can I enter via the King Hussein Bridge (Israel Border)?

Entry rules at land borders are stricter. You generally cannot get a Visa on Arrival at the King Hussein Bridge (Allenby) unless you have a pre-arranged authorization. We recommend flying into Amman (Queen Alia) or Aqaba.
