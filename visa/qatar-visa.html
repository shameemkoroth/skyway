Qatar Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Doha is no longer just a transit hub; it's a world-class destination blending modern luxury with deep traditions. For Indian citizens, entering Qatar is easy, but the rules can be confusing due to multiple visa types. Skyway Tours & Travels helps you navigate the Hayya Platform to ensure you choose the most cost-effective entry method.

💡 The "Hayya A1" Strategy (Highly Recommended)

Cost: 100 QAR (approx ₹2,300).
Why choose this? Unlike the free Visa Waiver, the Hayya A1 Entry Visa gives you the flexibility to book ANY hotel or stay with Family/Friends. You are NOT forced to book expensive hotels.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Hayya A1 Tourist Visa (Recommended)

This is a pre-approved entry visa applied for online.

  • Application: Online via the Hayya Portal.
  • Fee: 100 QAR.
  • Accommodation: Flexible. You can stay with family ("Host" option) or book any hotel (Booking.com, Agoda, etc.).
  • Validity: 30 Days (Single Entry).

2. Visa Waiver / On-Arrival (Conditional)

This is often called "Visa on Arrival," but technically it is a waiver.

  • Fee: Free of Charge.
  • Condition: You MUST hold a confirmed hotel reservation made EXCLUSIVELY through the Discover Qatar website for the entire duration of your stay.
  • Drawback: Discover Qatar hotels can be significantly more expensive than standard booking sites.

⚠️ Mandatory Health Insurance

All visitors to Qatar must purchase a Health Insurance Policy from a specific list of insurance companies registered with the Ministry of Public Health (MOPH).
Cost: Approx 50 QAR per month. Skyway Tours can issue this policy instantly for you.

Essential Documents Required

To process your Hayya A1 Visa through Skyway Tours, please provide:

  • Passport Scan: Bio-page valid for at least 3 months from arrival.
  • Photograph: High-quality recent photo (White background).
  • Accommodation: Hotel booking or Host's QID/Address (if staying with family).
  • Flight Ticket: Confirmed Return Ticket.

🕌 Jewels of Doha: Things to Do in Qatar

Experience the culture and modernity of the Pearl of the Gulf:

Souq Waqif

The beating heart of Doha. Wander through ancient alleyways smelling of spices and perfumes, visit the Falcon Hospital, and dine on authentic Qatari food.

National Museum of Qatar

Known as the "Desert Rose," this architectural marvel tells the story of Qatar's history from pearl diving to the oil boom.

Katara Cultural Village

A hub of art and culture featuring a stunning Greek-style amphitheater, the Golden Mosque, and high-end galleries.

The Pearl-Qatar

A man-made island of luxury. Walk along the marina lined with yachts, enjoy Mediterranean-style dining, or shop at luxury boutiques.

Museum of Islamic Art (MIA)

Designed by I.M. Pei, this iconic building sits on its own island. It houses one of the world's most complete collections of Islamic artifacts.

Desert Safari & Inland Sea

Take a 4x4 dune bashing tour to Khor Al Adaid (Inland Sea), one of the few places in the world where the desert dunes meet the ocean.

Common Questions

Can I enter with a US/UK Visa?

Yes. If you have a valid US, UK, Schengen, Canada, or Australia visa, you can apply for the Hayya A2 ETA. It is also 100 QAR but often processed faster.

What is the minimum cash requirement?

You should carry (or have on a valid credit card) at least 5,000 QAR (approx ₹1.1 Lakh) per family to prove financial solvency at immigration if asked.

Is the Discover Qatar hotel rule strictly enforced?

Yes. If you opt for the "Visa Waiver/On-Arrival" route, airlines will deny boarding if you do not present a hotel voucher specifically from the Discover Qatar website.
