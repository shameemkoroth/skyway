Planning to witness the Great Pyramids of Giza or cruise down the Nile? Egypt's visa policy for Indians can be confusing, with different rules for standard tourists and those holding Western visas. Visa Types for Indian Nationals

Understanding the correct visa category is critical. Applying for the wrong type (like an E-Visa when you are not eligible) can result in denied boarding.

1. Regular Tourist Visa (Sticker Visa) - Most Common

  • Who is this for? For all Indian citizens who do not hold a valid visa from the US, UK, Schengen, Japan, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand.
  • Process: This is a physical visa stamped into your passport. It cannot be done online. You must submit your physical passport and documents to us.
  • Validity: typically valid for 90 days.
  • Stay Duration: Allows a stay of up to 30 days.

2. Conditional Visa on Arrival (Special Eligibility)

  • Eligibility: You can only get a Visa on Arrival if you hold a valid, used/unused visa or residence permit from:
    • USA, UK, Schengen Countries, Canada, Japan, Australia, or New Zealand.
  • Requirement: The Western visa must be valid at the time of entry into Egypt.
  • Fee: Payable in USD Cash ($25 approx) at the bank kiosks before immigration counters.

3. Agent-Assisted Group E-Visa

  • Note: Standard individual E-Visas are generally NOT available to Indian citizens. However, if you are booking a complete tour package involving a guaranteed group via an authorized Egyptian agency, an E-Visa may be arranged. Contact us to see if your package qualifies.

Essential Documents Required (Sticker Visa)

For the Sticker Visa, we will compile your dossier for submission to the Consulate.

Mandatory Documents

  • Original Passport: Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the travel date and have at least two blank pages.
  • Photographs: 2 recent photos (3.5cm x 4.5cm) on a white background. (Matte finish preferred, 80% face coverage).
  • Bank Statements: Original updated bank statements for the last 6 months, stamped and signed by the bank. A minimum closing balance of approx INR 50,000 - 80,000 per person is recommended.
  • Cover Letter: A personal covering letter explaining the purpose of the visit and itinerary.

Supporting Documents

  • Flight Tickets: Confirmed return flight bookings.
  • Hotel Voucher: Confirmed hotel booking for the entire duration of stay.
  • Employment Proof: Leave letter from employer (NOC) or Salary Slips (for employees) / GST or Business Registration (for self-employed).

Processing Information

The sticker visa process is manual and involves embassy processing.

  • Processing Time: Typically takes 10 to 15 working days. We advise applying at least 3 weeks before your travel date.
  • Jurisdiction: Applications are processed at the Embassy in New Delhi or Consulate in Mumbai depending on your state of residence.

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Yellow Fever: A valid Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate is MANDATORY if you are arriving from or transiting through (for >12 hours) a country with risk of Yellow Fever transmission (e.g., Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda). If flying directly from India/Gulf, it is usually not required, but carry it if you have it.
  • Currency Restrictions:
    • Foreign Currency: You must declare any cash amount exceeding $10,000 USD (or equivalent) upon entry and exit.
    • Local Currency: You are generally not allowed to bring in or take out more than 5,000 Egyptian Pounds (EGP). It is best to carry USD and exchange locally.
  • Photography: Strictly avoid photographing government buildings, police checkpoints, or military personnel. It can lead to detention.

Common Questions

Can I apply for an E-Visa online myself?

If you hold an Indian passport without a US/UK/Schengen visa, NO. Many "fake" websites claim to offer this, but your application will likely be rejected or you will be denied boarding. Stick to the Sticker Visa.

Is the visa fee refundable?

No, embassy visa fees are non-refundable once the application is submitted.

Do I need to carry cash for the Visa on Arrival?

Yes, if you are eligible for the Conditional VOA (due to having a US/UK visa), you must pay the fee (approx $25) in Cash (USD). Credit cards are often not accepted at the bank kiosks in the arrival hall.
