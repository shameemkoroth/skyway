Angola Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Angola is a hidden jewel in Southern Africa, offering dramatic waterfalls, vast deserts, and a vibrant Portuguese-influenced culture. The best news? As of recent updates, Angola has significantly simplified entry for Indian tourists. Skyway Tours & Travels guides you through the new Visa-Free rules and ensures your paperwork is perfect for a smooth border crossing.

🎉 Great News: Visa-Free Entry!

Indian citizens traveling for tourism purposes are now Exempt from requiring a visa for stays of up to 30 Days per entry (Max 90 days per year). However, you must still meet strict entry requirements at the border.

Entry Rules for Indian Nationals

1. Tourist Entry (Visa-Free)

While you do not need a visa sticker or E-Visa for tourism, you MUST present the following to Immigration Officers upon arrival in Luanda:

  • Valid Passport: Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your date of entry.
  • Return Flight Ticket: Confirmed booking showing you will leave Angola.
  • Accommodation Proof: Hotel booking confirmation or Letter of Invitation.
  • Yellow Fever Certificate: Original vaccination card (Mandatory).
  • Means of Subsistence: Proof of sufficient funds (e.g., cash or credit cards) to cover your stay.

2. Business / Work Visa

If you are traveling for business meetings, conferences, or employment, the Visa-Free rule does NOT apply. You must apply for a proper visa.

  • Process: Requires an invitation letter from an Angolan company.
  • Application: Can be processed via the official SME E-Visa portal or the Embassy.

⚠️ Mandatory Health Requirement

Yellow Fever Vaccination

Angola enforces strict health controls. You must carry your original International Certificate of Vaccination (Yellow Card) showing a valid Yellow Fever shot taken at least 10 days before travel. Failure to produce this will result in denied entry.

🇦🇴 The Giant of Africa: Main Attractions

Angola offers raw, untouched beauty. Here are the must-visit locations:

Kalandula Falls

One of the largest waterfalls in Africa by volume. Located in Malanje province, these horseshoe-shaped falls are a breathtaking natural wonder.

Luanda & The Marginal

Walk the beautiful seaside promenade (Marginal) in the capital. Visit the Fortress of São Miguel for history and panoramic city views.

Miradouro da Lua

"Viewpoint of the Moon." A stunning landscape of tri-colored cliffs eroded by wind and rain, resembling the lunar surface. Located 40km south of Luanda.

Quiçama National Park

The only functioning safari park near Luanda. Spot giraffes, zebras, and elephants on a river safari or game drive.

Tundavala Gap (Lubango)

A spectacular escarpment where the high plateau drops 1,000 meters down to the desert plains. The views are dizzying and unforgettable.

Serra da Leba Pass

A famous winding mountain road with zigzag hairpin turns. It is an engineering marvel and one of the most photographed spots in the country.

Common Questions

Is it safe to travel to Angola?

Yes, major cities and tourist areas are generally safe. However, avoid walking alone at night in Luanda and always stick to official tourist guides in remote areas.

What currency should I take?

The local currency is the Kwanza (AOA). It is highly recommended to carry USD or Euros (cash) to exchange, as international cards may not work everywhere.

Do I need a visa for a 2-day trip?

No. Under the new rules, Indian tourists are visa-exempt for stays up to 30 days. You just need your passport and supporting travel docs.
