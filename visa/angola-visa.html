Visa Options for Indian Nationals

Angola has recently updated its travel policies to boost tourism. Understanding the correct category is crucial for a smooth entry.

1. Tourist Visa (E-Visa / Exemption)

  • Status: India is currently among the countries benefiting from relaxed tourist entry norms (often Visa-Free for stays up to 30 days), but strict documentation is required at the border.
  • Purpose: Tourism, holiday, and visiting family.
  • Validity: Allows a stay of up to 30 days per entry (max 90 days per year).
  • Processing: Pre-travel registration or E-Visa application is highly recommended to avoid issues at airline counters.

2. Business Visa

  • Purpose: For attending conferences, meetings, or exploring trade opportunities.
  • Requirement: Requires an invitation letter from an entity based in Angola.

Essential Documents Required

Angola has strict health and financial entry requirements. Skyway Tours will verify your file to ensure you are not denied boarding.

Mandatory Documents

  • Passport Scan: Must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry and have at least two blank pages.
  • Yellow Fever Certificate: CRITICAL: An International Certificate of Vaccination against Yellow Fever is mandatory for entry into Angola. You must carry the original booklet.
  • Photograph: Recent passport-sized color photograph (white background).

Supporting Documents (Immigration Check)

To ensure smooth entry at Luanda (LAD) International Airport, you must have:

  • Flight Tickets: Confirmed return or onward journey tickets.
  • Hotel Booking: Confirmed proof of accommodation or a notarized invitation letter from a host in Angola.
  • Proof of Livelihood: You may be asked to show sufficient funds (e.g., USD or Credit Cards) to cover your daily expenses during your stay.

Processing Information

Our team assists in compiling your dossier to meet the standards of the Migration and Foreigners Service (SME).

  • Processing Time: Standard processing typically takes 3 to 5 working days.
  • Pre-Check Service: We review your Yellow Fever cards and passport data before submission to prevent common errors.

Important Traveler Advisory

  • Health Precautions: Apart from Yellow Fever, malaria prophylaxis is strongly recommended. Consult your doctor before travel.
  • Language: The official language is Portuguese. English is not widely spoken outside major hotels. Having a guided tour or translator is helpful.
  • Currency Restriction: There are strict limits on the amount of local currency (Kwanza) you can take out of the country. It is best to carry USD/EUR and exchange locally.

Common Questions

Is the Yellow Fever vaccine really mandatory?

Yes. You will likely be denied boarding in India or entry in Angola without a valid original Yellow Fever vaccination card.

Can I get a Visa on Arrival?

While provisions exist, "Visa on Arrival" in Angola usually requires a pre-approval certificate obtained online before you fly. Flying without this pre-approval is risky.

What is the best currency to carry?

USD (United States Dollars) printed after 2013 are the most widely accepted foreign currency for exchange. Credit cards are accepted in major hotels but cash is king for local shops.
