Malaysia Entry Guide for Indian Travellers

From the glittering towers of Kuala Lumpur to the rainforests of Borneo, Malaysia is a favorite destination for Indian families. Skyway Tours & Travels brings you the latest travel news: Indian citizens can currently enter Malaysia Visa-Free! However, a new digital requirement (MDAC) is now mandatory for all visitors.

🎉 Visa-Free Entry (Extended!)

Good News: The Visa-Free Entry program for Indian citizens has been officially extended until December 31, 2026. You do NOT need to apply for a visa for stays up to 30 days.

Entry Requirements for Indian Nationals

While the "Visa" is waived, immigration checks are strict. You must have the following ready:

⚠️ The "MDAC" Requirement (Mandatory)

All foreign travelers must complete the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC).
Deadline: You must submit this form online 3 days prior to your arrival. If you arrive without it, you may be denied entry.

Document Checklist for Immigration

  • Passport: Must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry.
  • MDAC PDF: A printed or digital copy of the Digital Arrival Card email acknowledgment.
  • Confirmed Return Ticket: Mandatory proof that you will leave Malaysia within 30 days.
  • Accommodation Proof: Confirmed hotel booking or address of host.
  • Sufficient Funds: Carry approx $100 USD (or 500 Ringgit) per day of stay in Cash/Cards to show if asked.

How to Fill the MDAC (Step-by-Step)

Skyway Tours can assist you with filling this form accurately to avoid data mismatch errors at the e-gates.

  1. Visit Portal: Go to the official Immigration Department website.
  2. Enter Details: Fill in your passport data, date of arrival, and accommodation address.
  3. Submit: Once submitted, you will receive a PIN / Acknowledgement email.
  4. Save PDF: Download the PIN email. You might not need to show it if you use the Autogate, but keeping a copy is essential.

What about E-Visa?

You only need to apply for a paid E-Visa if you plan to stay more than 30 days. For standard tourism (under 30 days), the Visa-Free route is the best choice.

🏙️ Top Attractions & Things to Do

Planning your Malaysian itinerary? Here are the unmissable spots:

Petronas Twin Towers (KL)

The world's tallest twin structures. Walk the Skybridge on the 41st floor for panoramic views of Kuala Lumpur.

Batu Caves

Climb the colorful 272 steps past the massive golden Lord Murugan statue to reach the limestone cathedral cave temples.

Genting Highlands

A "City of Entertainment" in the clouds. Enjoy the cool weather, casinos, indoor/outdoor theme parks, and the scenic cable car ride.

Langkawi Sky Bridge

Take the cable car to this curved pedestrian bridge suspended 700m above sea level, offering views of the Andaman Sea and Thailand.

Penang (George Town)

A UNESCO World Heritage site famous for its interactive street art, colonial architecture, and the best street food in Malaysia.

Cameron Highlands

Escape the heat in these rolling green hills covered in tea plantations, strawberry farms, and colonial-style cottages.

Common Questions

Can I use the Autogates at KL Airport?

Yes! Indian citizens who have successfully registered their MDAC 3 days prior can now use the Autogates at KLIA1 and KLIA2 for faster immigration clearance.

Is the visa-free entry extendable?

No. The 30-day Social Visit Pass is generally non-extendable. You must exit Malaysia on or before the 30th day. Overstaying attracts heavy fines and blacklisting.

Do children need MDAC?

Yes, every passenger regardless of age (including infants) requires their own MDAC submission.
