Tajikistan Visa Guide for Indian Travellers

Tajikistan is the adventure capital of Central Asia, home to the legendary Pamir Highway and crystal-clear alpine lakes. For Indian citizens, entry is straightforward via the E-Visa system. Skyway Tours & Travels ensures your application includes the critical GBAO Permit so you don't get turned back at military checkpoints in the mountains.

🚀 E-Visa Only (No Visa on Arrival)

Crucial Update: There is NO Visa on Arrival for Indian citizens at Dushanbe Airport. You must apply for and receive your E-Visa before you board your flight.

Visa Types for Indian Nationals

1. Tourist E-Visa (Standard)

This is the primary visa for all tourists.

  • Validity: The visa is valid for 90 days from the date of issue.
  • Duration of Stay: You can stay for a maximum of 60 Days.
  • Entry Type: Single or Multiple Entry options available.

2. The "GBAO Permit" (Mandatory for Pamirs)

⚠️ Visiting the Pamir Highway?

If you plan to travel to the Gorno-Badakhshan Autonomous Region (GBAO)—which includes the Pamir Highway and Wakhan Valley—you MUST select the GBAO Permit add-on when applying for your E-Visa.
Without this specific permit on your visa, you will be denied entry at military checkpoints.

Essential Documents Required

To process your E-Visa through Skyway Tours, we need:

  • Passport Scan: Clear color scan of the bio-page (Valid for 6 months).
  • Photograph: Digital passport-style photo (White background).
  • Itinerary: Rough travel plan (required to justify GBAO permit request).

⛰️ Roof of the World: Top Things to Do

Tajikistan is for the bold. Here are the unmissable experiences:

The Pamir Highway (M41)

The world's second-highest international highway. Drive through moon-like landscapes, high-altitude deserts, and pass by yaks grazing at 4,000 meters.

Iskanderkul Lake

Named after Alexander the Great, this stunning turquoise lake in the Fann Mountains is perfect for hiking and relaxing in nature.

Dushanbe

The green capital city. Visit the National Museum, see the world's second-tallest flagpole, and stroll through Rudaki Park.

The Seven Lakes (Haft Kul)

A chain of seven vertically arranged lakes, each a different color from mesmerizing turquoise to deep purple. Great for a day hike from Penjikent.

Wakhan Valley

Drive along the border river with Afghanistan views just across the water. Explore ancient Silk Road fortresses like Yamchun Fort and soak in hot springs.

Khujand

One of the oldest cities in Central Asia. Visit the bustling Panjshanbe Bazaar and the historical fortress by the Syr Darya river.

Common Questions

Do I need to register after arrival?

Yes. If you stay in Tajikistan for more than 10 Days, you must register with the OVIR (migration police) within that period. Hotels often do not do this automatically; you may need to go to the office yourself with our guidance.

Can I use Indian Debit/Credit cards?

Generally, No. Indian cards (RuPay/standard Visa) often fail in Tajik ATMs. It is highly recommended to carry USD or Euro Cash (pristine notes, no tears/marks) to exchange for Tajik Somoni.

Is the border with Kyrgyzstan open?

Land borders between Tajikistan and Kyrgyzstan are frequently closed due to political tensions. Always check the latest status with us before planning a cross-border trip.
