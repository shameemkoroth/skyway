Package Inclusions

Our standard Umrah package is all-inclusive, ensuring no hidden costs:

  • Visa: Complete Umrah visa processing and documentation.
  • Flights: Return economy class air tickets.
  • Transfers: Luxury AC bus transportation for Jeddah-Makkah-Madinah transfers.
  • Ziyarah: Guided tours to historical sites in Makkah and Madinah (Quba, Uhud, Jabal al-Nour, etc.).
  • Umrah Kit: Complimentary hand bag, shoulder bag, and ID card.
  • Support: 24/7 ground support from our Saudi team.
  • Zamzam: Assistance in arranging Zamzam water (subject to airline regulations).

Typical 15-Day Itinerary

While itineraries may vary based on flight schedules, here is a typical schedule for our 15-day group package:

  1. Day 1: Departure
    Assemble at the airport in Kerala. Briefing by the group leader. Flight to Jeddah/Madinah.
  2. Day 2-5: Madinah Al Munawwarah
    Stay in Madinah. Daily prayers at Masjid An-Nabawi. Ziyarah to historical sites including Masjid Quba and Mount Uhud.
  3. Day 6: Journey to Makkah
    Travel to Makkah by luxury bus. Stop at Miqat for Ihram. Perform the first Umrah upon arrival under the guidance of our Amir.
  4. Day 7-14: Makkah Al Mukarramah
    Spiritual days in Makkah. Optional additional Umrahs (Tana'im/Ja'rana). Ziyarah to Arafat, Mina, and Muzdalifah (visits only).
  5. Day 15: Return
    Tawaf al-Wada (Farewell Tawaf). Transfer to Jeddah airport for the return flight to Kerala.

Documents Required for Booking

To reserve your seat, please ensure you have the following:

  • Original Passport with minimum 6 months validity from the date of travel.
  • 2 Passport size photographs (White background).
  • Pan Card copy (for Indian government tax compliance).
  • Advance payment for seat confirmation.
